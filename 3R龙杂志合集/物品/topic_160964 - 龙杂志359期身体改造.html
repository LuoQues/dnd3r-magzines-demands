战斗模组COMBAT MOD

Combat body modifications take two general forms.

战斗身体改造有两种一般形式。

Weapon: A fighter might choose to have a punch dagger permanently emerging from the skin above his wrist or a monk might elect to install silver and cold iron caps on her knuckles (which count as silver or cold iron gauntlets). Weapon combat mods cost double the weapon's non-magical cost (include masterwork and special material costs, but not costs for any magical enhancements), Only light and one-handed weapons can be attached, and only to the subject's arms. Weapons attached in this way are used normally, as manufactured-weapon attacks. Implanted weap-ons themselves function normally, although the wielder cannot use them two-handed. Weapon combatmods cannot be disarmed or dropped (but they can be sundered normally) and they are always considered drawn.

Attaching a bludgeoning weapon has a Craft (body modifications) or Heal DCof 20. A piercing weapon is DC 25, while a slashing weapon's DC is 30.

武器：战士可以选择让匕首永久地从手腕上方的皮肤中伸出，或者武僧可以选择在指关节上覆盖银和寒铁（算作银或寒铁手套）。武器战斗模组的成本是武器非魔法花费的两倍（包括精制和特殊材料花费，但不包括任何魔法增强的花费），只能连接轻型和单手武器，并且只能连接到主体的手臂上。以这种方式连接的武器通常用作人造武器攻击。植入的武器本身可以正常工作，尽管持有者不能双手使用它们。武器战斗模组不能被卸除武装或丢弃（但它们可以正常击破），并且它们始终被视为出鞘。

安装钝击武器的手艺 (身体改造) 或医疗 DC 为 20。穿刺武器的 DC 为 25，而挥砍武器的 DC 为 30。

Armor: Much less common is the permanent application of armor, in which the crafter magically burns and warps flesh to adhere to small steel plates. A character wishing to gird himselfin this manner gains an armor bonus up to +8, at a cost of 300 gp and 5 pounds of encumbrance per point of armor bonus. Armor combat mod has a maximum Dex bonus of 0 minus the armor bonus, an armor check penalty equal to the armor bonus (masterwork armor reduces this penalty by 1 as normal), and an arcane spell failure chance of 5% per point of armor bonus. Up to a +3 armor bonus counts as light armor and allows normal speed, an armor bonus of +4 to +6 counts as medium armor, and an armor bonus of +7 or+8 counts as heavy armor.

This armor can be spiked, constructed from special materials, and made magical, all for the normal costs of such treatments. Removing an armor combat mod deals ad6 points of damage per level or HD of the character and renders the plates useless. Characters choosing this modification may not wear any other armor, although they may still use shields.

Attaching light armor requires a DC 20 Craft (body modifications) or Heal check. Medium armor has a DC of 25, while heavy armor's DC is 30.

Due to the painful nature of both these operations and the potential for social stigma, most who undergo these procedures wait until they can afford to use high-quality magical weapons or materials. Note that most lawful or good civilized places frown upon combat mods and characters with them invite extra scrutiny from law enforcement.

盔甲：不太常见的是永久使用盔甲，即工匠用魔法灼烧和变形肉体，使其粘附在小钢板上。希望以这种方式武装自己的角色将获得最高 +8 的盔甲加值，每点盔甲加值需要花费 300 gp 和 5 磅的负重。盔甲战斗模组的最大敏捷加值为 09减去盔甲加值，盔甲检定减值等于盔甲加值（精制盔甲正常情况下会将此减值减少 1），每点盔甲加值奥术法术失败率为 5%。最高 +3 的盔甲加值计为轻型盔甲并允许正常速度，+4 至 +6 的盔甲加值计为中型盔甲，+7 或 +8 的护甲加值计为重型盔甲。

这种盔甲可以带刺、用特殊材料制成，也可以施加魔法，所有这些都需要正常治疗费用。移除盔甲战斗模组会造成角色每级或 HD 的 1d6 点伤害，并使盔甲板失效。选择此改造的角色不得穿戴任何其他盔甲，但他们仍可使用盾牌。

安装轻型盔甲需要 DC 20 手艺（身体改造）或医疗检定。中型盔甲的 DC 为 25，而重型盔甲的 DC 为 30。

由于这两种操作的痛苦性质以及可能带来的社会耻辱，大多数接受这些手术的人都会等到他们有能力使用高品质的魔法武器或材料。请注意，大多数守序或文明的地方都反对战斗模组，使用它们的角色会受到执法部门的额外审查。

额外身体部位EXTRA BODY SLOT

Although viewed as extreme by many, body modification is occasionally used to give a character access to an additional magic item body slot. A small wondrous item (no larger than dagger) is inserted into an incision as far from other body slots as possible, generally beneath the ribs or along the thigh. The item functions normally even if another item already occupies its normal body slot. This procedure, however, has grave drawbacks, as the overlapping and conflicting magical fields stress the body and reduce the subject's Constitution score by 2 points for as long as the item remains within the character's body. Only rings, rods, and wondrous items crafted specifically for this purpose can be used. They cost 1-1/2 times the normal price. Removing an item from an extra body slot requires a procedure that deals 1d6 points of damage per level or HD of the character.

Implanting an extra body slot requires a DC 30 Craft (body modifications) or Heal check.

尽管许多人认为身体改造是极端做法，但有时也会使用身体改造让角色获得额外的魔法物品身体部位。将一个小巧的魔法物品（不大于匕首）嵌入尽可能远离其他身体部位的切口，一般在肋骨下方或大腿上。即使另一个物品已经占据了其正常身体槽，该物品也能正常发挥作用。然而，这一过程存在严重缺陷，因为重叠和冲突的魔法场会给身体带来压力，只要物品留在角色体内，就会使对象的体质属性降低 2 点。只能使用专门为此目的制作的戒指、权杖和奇物。它们的价格是正常价格的 1-1/2 倍。从额外的身体部位中移除物品需要进行一个过程，该过程对角色的每个等级或 HD 造成 1d6 点伤害。

植入额外身体部位需要进行 DC 30 手艺（身体改造）或医疗检定。
