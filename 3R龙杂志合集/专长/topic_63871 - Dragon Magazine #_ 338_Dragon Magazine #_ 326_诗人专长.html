FOCUSED PERFORMER [GENERAL]

Your mastery allows you to finish your performance even when surrounded by distractions.

Prerequisite: Concentration 1 rank, Perform 4 ranks, bardic music class ability.

Benefit: When required to make a Concentration check to maintain an arcane spell with a verbal component you may use your Perform skill instead. You cannot use this ability on a silent spell.

Normal: A character uses Concentration to cast spells under duress.

专注表演者（FOCUSED PERFORMER） [普通专长]

你的控制力让你即使是受到周围的干扰也能完成你的表演。

先决条件：专注技能1级，表演技能4级，吟唱（Bardic Music）职业能力

效果：当需要做专注检定以维持一个包含语言成分的法术时，你可以使用您的表演技能来代替。你不能在一个搭配法术默发的法术上使用此能力。

正常：一个角色在被胁迫时是使用专注技能释放法术。

Suggested Class/Race: Bard

FOCUSED PERFORMANCE [BARDIC MUSIC]

You can modify your bardic music by channeling it backinto itself

Prerequisite: Concentration 1 rank, Perform 6 ranks, Focused Performer, bardic music class ability.

Benefit: You can expend daily uses of your bardic music ability to modify your performance. You must succeed at a Perform check (DC below) to successfully modify your bardic music ability. You may only apply one modification to a use of bardic music.

BARDIC MUSIC MODIFICATIONS

The most popular bardic music modifications follow.

Accompaniment: Spend three daily uses of bardic music to perform two abilities at the same time. You must succeed at a Perform check every round or else you prematurely lose the rhythm and end both abilities early. Each round you may choose to stop using one ability and maintain  the other as per the standard rules for bardic music. You may use this modification with all bardic music abilities. DC 20 + highest Perform ranks required.

Dramatic Pause: While maintaining a bardic music ability you make a check to pause your performance and take another action (such as casting spell, making an attack, and so on). The following round you must resume the performance as though you never stopped, but you may  attempt another dramatic pause in following rounds. Each dramatic pause expends two daily uses of bardic music. You may use this modification with fascinate, inspire courage, inspire greatness, and inspire heroics. DC 20 + Perform ranks required.

Harmony: You may spend an extra use of bardic music to allow an ability that normally affects only one creature to affect two creatures instead. You may use this modification with inspire competence, suggestion, and song of freedom. DC 25 + Perform ranks required.

Individual Performance: You may spend an extra use of bardic music to target a single individual (other than yourself) with an ability affecting multiple targets. If affected, the target receives double the bonus or penalty. You may use this modification with fascinate, inspire courage, inspire greatness, and inspire heroics. DC 15 + Perform ranks required.

  Projection: Spend two daily uses of bardic music to double the area of effect of the ability you activate next. You may use this modification with countersong, fascinate, inspire competence, inspire greatness, inspire heroics, and song of freedom. DC 10 + Perform ranks required.

  Rhythm: If you maintain an ability for 3 or more rounds and expend an extra daily use of bardic music you cause the ability you're using to last an additional 1 rounds after you stop performing. Make the Perform check when you've stopped maintaining the ability. You may use this modification with fascinate, inspire competence, inspire courage, inspire greatness, and inspire heroics, DC 10 + Perform ranks required.

  Riveting Performance: By spending an extra daily use of bardic music, all targets in range who hear you are affected regardless of line of sight. You may use this modification with fascinate and inspire competence. DC 15+ Perform ranks required.

专注表演(FOCUSED PERFORMANCE) [吟唱]

你可以通过修改你的吟唱能力来重新引导它。

先决条件：专注技能1级，表演技能6级，专注表演者（FOCUSED PERFORMER，龙杂志338专长）, 吟唱（Bardic Music）职业能力

效果：你可以消耗你的每日吟唱能力使用次数来修改你的表演。你必须通过成功的表演检定（DC下文）来成功地修改你的吟唱能力。你的一个吟唱只能应用一个修改。

吟唱修改：

最流行的吟唱修改如下。

伴奏（Accompaniment）：消耗3次每日吟唱次数可以同时演奏使用两种能力。每一轮你必须通过成功的表演检定否则你过早的失去节奏从而提前提前结束2种能力。每回合你可以选择停止使用一种能力并维持另一个如同按照吟唱的标准规则。你可以使用此能力修改所有的吟唱能力。DC20+最高的必要表演技能等级

戏剧性暂停（Dramatic Pause）：当维持吟唱能力时你可以进行一个检定以暂停你的表演并进行其他动作（如施展法术，进行攻击，等等）。下一轮你必须恢复表演就像你从来没有停止过，但你下回合可以尝试另一个戏剧性暂停。每一次戏剧性暂停消耗2个每日吟唱次数。你可以使用此能力修改迷魂，激发勇气，提振战力和激发豪情。DC20+必要的表演技能等级。

和声（Harmony）：你可以消耗一个额外的吟唱次数让一个通常只影响一个生物的能力由影响两个生物来代替。你可以使用此能力修改提振技能,暗示和自由歌。DC25+必要的表演技能等级。

个人表演（ Individual Performance）：你可以消耗一个额外的吟唱次数针对单个个体（除你自己以外）受到如同影响多个目标的能力。如果受到影响，目标受到双倍加值或罚值。你可以使用此能力修改迷魂，激发勇气，提振战力和激发豪情。DC15+必要的表演技能等级。

投影（Projection）：消耗2次每日吟唱次数可以让你使用能力影响的区域接下来成为双倍。下次你可以使用此能力修改破咒曲，迷魂，提振技能,提振战力，激发豪情，和自由歌。DC10+必要的表演技能等级。

韵律（Rhythm）：如果你维持一个能力3轮或更多轮并额外消耗一个额外的吟唱次数你在停止表演后该能力的持续时间增加1轮。做一个表演检定当你停止维持能力时。你可以使用此能力修改迷魂，提振技能，激发勇气，提振战力和激发豪情，DC10+必要的表演技能等级。

诱人表演（Riveting Performance）：通过消耗一个额外的每日吟唱次数，范围内的所有能听见你声音的目标无需视线效果都会受到影响。你可以使用此能力修改迷魂和提振技能。DC15+必要的表演技能等级。

Suggested Class/Race: Bard

CHAOS MUSIC [ANARCHIC]

   The power of chaos improves your bardic music ability. Your performance becomes frenetic and completely discordant. Something about it speaks to some forgotten aspect of your primal consciousness.

  Prerequisites: Perform 4 ranks, bardic music ability.

  Benefit: Your effective bard level increases by +4 for the purposes of determining which bardic music abilities you can use. This feat can't increase your effective bard level beyond your Hit Dice. However, if you can't benefit from the full bonus immediately, but you later gain nonbard Hit Dice, you may apply the rest of the bonus.

   For example, a human 5th-level bard/3rd-level fighter who selects this feat would increase his effective bard level from 5th to 8th (since he has 8 HD), allowing him to use the suggestion ability and increasing his inspire courage bonus to +2. If he later gained another nonbard level, he would gain the remainder of the bonus and his effective bard level for bardic music abilities would become 9th (since he now has 9 HD).

   This does not affect your spells per day or spells known. It only increases your effective bard level in regards to using your bardic music ability.

混乱音乐（CHAOS MUSIC） [ANARCHIC：无政府主义者]

混乱之力提高你的吟唱能力。你的表现变得疯狂并且完全失调。你说出一些被你遗忘的原始意识之事。

先决条件：表演技能4级，吟唱（Bardic Music）职业能力

效果：你的有效吟游诗人等级+4来确定你可用的吟游诗人吟唱能力。这个专长提供给你的有效吟游诗人等级不会超过你的HD。但是，如果你获得的额外加值不是满值，但你之后又获得了不是吟游诗人职业的HD，你可以获得剩余的奖励加值。

举例来说，一个人类是5级诗人/3级战士他选择这个专长会使他的有效吟游诗人等级从5级变为8级（因为他有8个HD），这使他能使用暗示（Suggestion）能力且提高他的激发勇气能力（Inspire Courage）为+2。如果之后他再获得非吟游诗人的等级。他会得到剩余的奖励加值并且他的吟唱能力有效吟游诗人等级成为9级（因为现在它有9个HD）。

这个效果不会影响你每天的法术和你所知的法术，他只增加你使用吟游诗人音乐能力时的有效吟游诗人等级。
