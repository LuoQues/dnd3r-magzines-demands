背景：灰墙

by Keith Baker

卓姆是科瓦雷大陆上最奇怪的地方之一，一片怪物之地，是美杜莎和牛头人的家园。石像鬼和双足飞龙在天空盘旋，地精和狼人潜伏在森林里。几个世纪以来，它一直是一个未开化的边境地带。直到索拉•科伦的女儿们把它塑造成一个国家。外界很少有人认为这个怪物的联盟能存在超过一年，但他到现在已经延续了十多年。通过巨人的强壮力量和美杜莎的巧妙石工，城镇在荒原上拔地而起。其中一个城镇位于卓姆和布鲁兰的边境上，在灰墙山脉下建成并因此而得名。这就是灰墙，怪物之城，通往卓姆的门户。

DROAAM, one of the strangest places on Khorvaire, is a land of monsters that serves as a home to medusas and minotaurs. Gargoyles and wyverns circle in the skies, and goblins and werewolves lurk in the forests. For centuries it has been an untamed frontier. Then the Daughters of Sora Kell forged it into a nation. Few in the outer world expected this monstrous alliance to survive a year, but it has endured for over a decade. Towns are rising in the wastelands, built with the strength of giants and the cunning stonework of the medusas. One such town lies on the border of Droaam and Breland, carved from the foothills of the mountains from which it takes its name. This is Graywall, town of monsters and gateway to Droaam.

为什么要来灰墙？

虽然比萨恩或风暴湾要小，但灰墙是一个独特的小镇，为冒险家们提供了许多机会。

一个自由城镇：卓姆不仅不是五国之一，它甚至不受王座堡条约的约束。因此，东方的法律在这里没有任何意义，这使它成为强盗、逃兵和各种战犯的避难所。它不受伽利法法典的约束，也是东部地区违禁品的来源，从神秘的麻醉剂到来自安黛尔或布鲁兰的库存军火走私。冒险家可能不得不把一个逃犯从怪物之城救出来，阻止翡翠利爪的代理人购买奥术武器，或者找到一个黑市商人来做对他们有利的交易。

达坎遗址：卓姆保存着未被人类双手触及的秘密。自从护门者把异变魔赶到了地底深处之后，这些遗迹便空空荡荡的，而另外的那些地下城则是被那些自然的怪物，异怪还有不死生物们组成的怪物大军所占据。灰墙可以成为寻找"怪物时代"秘密的冒险家的优质行动基地。

家，令人讨厌的家：在第四版中，许多怪物种族成为优秀的玩家角色。牛头人、豺狼人、狗头人、地精和兽人都出现在第四版怪物图鉴中，变形怪和提夫林在卓姆生活会比在文明国家更容易。如果一组人选择扮演一个完全由怪物角色组成的小队，灰墙可能成为他们的故乡和行动基地，从这里他们可以探索达坎遗迹、与布鲁兰边防军交战，并卷入卓姆军阀们的阴谋之中。或者，现在与人类同伴一起在科瓦雷漫游的牛头人可能仍然会记得灰墙镇是他的家，当一个仇人攻击他的氏族时，他可能会被吸引回去。如果玩家角色被判处了莫须有的罪名，灰墙是一个可信而安全（尽管令人讨厌）的家。

WHY COME TO GRAYWALL?

Though smaller than Sharn or Stormreach, Graywall is a unique town with much to offer to adventurers.

A Free Town: Not only is Droaam not one of the Five Nations—it isn't even bound by the Treaty of Thronehold. As such, the laws of the east have no weight here, making it a haven for bandits, deserters, and all manner of war criminals. Free from the Code of Galifar, it is also a source for dangerous goods banned in the eastern lands, from mystical narcotics to surplus weapons of war smuggled from Aundair or Breland. Adventurers might have to pry a fugitive out from the town of monsters, prevent an agent of the Emerald Claw from making an deal for arcane armaments, or find a shady fence to make their own lucrative bargain.

Ruins of Dhakaan: Droaam holds secrets still untouched by human hands. Ruins have lain empty since the Gatekeepers drove the daelkyr into the depths . . . along with other dungeons held by monstrous forces—natural, aberrant, and undead. Graywall can make an excellent base of operations for adventurers searching for the secrets of the Age of Monsters.

Home, Unsavory Home: In 4th edition, many monstrous races make excellent player characters. Minotaurs, gnolls, kobolds, goblinoids, and orcs are all covered in the 4th edition Monster Manual, and doppelgangers and tieflings have an easier time in Droaam than they do in the civilized nations.If a group chooses to play a fully monstrous party, Graywall might be their home town and base of operations, from which they explore Dhakaani ruins, clash with Brelish border guards, and get drawn into the intrigues of Droaam's many warlords. Alternatively, the minotaur who now wanders across Khorvaire with his human comrades might still remember Graywall as his home, and be drawn back when an old enemy strikes his clan. And if the PCs are convicted of a crime they didn't commit, Graywall can prove a safe (if unsavory) home.

在灰墙的生活

卓姆是一个年轻的国家。仅仅30年前，灰墙还是一片破败的达坎遗迹。在过去的几十年里，一个国家在荒野中扎下了根，而灰墙已经成为人类王国和怪物之地之间的边界。在终末之战，它成为了怪物力量的集结地，既是萨拉释克家族的雇佣兵，也是袭击布鲁兰的战斗团队。随着战争的结束，索拉·科伦的女儿们投入了大量资源，在五国的边界上建立了一个强大的存在。因此，灰墙是一个蓬勃发展的城镇。扩建永不停止，因为一队队食人魔、兽人和美杜莎组成的团队在建造兵营、加固城墙。食人魔开采和运输石材，而狗头人和地精在拜拾克金矿场挖掘，在工厂和天地里劳作。豺狼人猎人从周围的荒野中带回兽皮和肉。还有东方的人——土匪、逃兵、商人等等，他们都在寻找机会。

生活在灰墙的大多数怪物都受雇于索拉•科伦的女儿们。这种服务的回报是食物、住所和一种自豪感，还有配给的苦酒。这是一种艰苦的生活，但比他们中多数人几十年来的生活要好得多——尤其是对地精来说，他们通常都被更强大的生物所压迫。近年来，外来者把伽利法的黄金带到了这座城镇，这既孕育了为外来者提供服务的行业，也催生了对他们进行抢劫的罪犯。

During the Last War it became a staging ground for monstrous forces, both those serving as mercenaries with House Tharashk and the warbands raiding Breland. With the war's end, the Daughters of Sora Kell have devoted significant resources to establishing a strong presence on the border of the Five Nations. As a result, Graywall is a booming town. The expansion never ends, since teams of ogres, orcs, and medusas work on new barracks and reinforce the fortress walls. Ogres quarry and shift stone, while kobolds and goblins delve in the byeshk mines and toil in the workhouses and fields. Gnoll hunters bring hides and meat in from the surrounding wilds. And then there are the people of the east—bandits, deserters, merchants, and more, all looking for opportunities.

The majority of the monsters that live in Graywall are in the employ of the Daughters of Sora Kell. The rewards for this service are food, shelter, and a sense of pride, along with rations of bitter grog. It's a hard life, but better than many of them would have had decades ago—especially for the goblins, who were typically subjugated by more powerful creatures. In recent years foreigners have brought the gold of Galifar to the town, giving birth both to services catering to the outlanders . . . and to criminals who prey upon them.

本地习俗

大部分居住于灰墙的怪物都在为卓姆效力。索拉•卡夏致力于激励她的人民，使他们愿意为创造一个更好的卓姆而努力。但在鼓励不那么好使时，强制手段就会被采用。每个工作队都有两个监工，一个是牛头人，一个是人面鸟妖。当采用蛮力时，牛头人充当铁拳。当工人们获准休息时，人面鸟妖呼唤他们工作，并为他们唱歌来提供娱乐。一个人面鸟妖监工使用标准的人面鸟妖数据，但替换潜行技能为交涉+12和洞察+10【译注：好像是4E的内容，我没看懂，去翻了4e怪物图鉴，没发现人面鸟妖harpy有潜行Stealth】。牛头人监工可能是牛头人战士或牛头人秘术师，这取决于他所负责的团队；无论是哪个，他都将获得+3的威吓检定加值。工人通常每天进行11个小时的轮班，再进行1小时的民兵演练。如果可以选择的话，城镇的大多数居民都是夜行的；然而，工作场所和矿井却全天候运转。

虽然卓姆的居民在理论上是平等的，但数个世纪的传统和流血已经使某些习惯深入人心。在街上，怪物通常会顺从更强大的生物，而大多数居民对这种等级制度有很好的认识。地精会从食人魔的路上走开，而食人魔会避让巨魔。这通常根据大小决定，但也有明确的例外。即使是巨魔也会回避路上的美杜莎。

五国的人们可能会觉得身边常有致命的生物的存在令人不安。

虽然他们相对罕见，但美杜莎会出现在灰墙的街头。女性美杜莎闭着眼睛，通过她的蛇发观察世界；没有这种选择的男性美杜莎通常会将一条纱布松散地系在他的眼睛上，以保护他人免受他致命的凝视。但是，愤怒的美杜莎很容易把纱布拆掉，人面鸟妖也能提高她的嗓音。实践中，这种情况很少发生；艾克索切莱尔对无缘无故杀人的怪物没有耐心，而卓姆的人知道，不要激怒他们致命的同胞。美杜莎可以用凝视杀人，就像萨恩的法师可以向人群扔火球一样。但除非被激怒，否则两者都不太可能这样做。

Common Customs

Most of the monstrous inhabitants of Graywall serve Droaam. Sora Katra strives to inspire her people, so that they want to work to create a stronger Droaam— but where inspiration fails, force is employed. Every work crew has two overseers, a minotaur and a harpy. The minotaur serves as the iron fist when brute force is employed. The harpy calls the team to work and entertains them with her song whenever they are granted a period of rest. A harpy overseer uses the standard harpy statistics, but replaces Stealth with Diplomacy +12 and Insight +10. A minotaur overseer might be a soldier or cabalist depending on the group he is minding; either way he receives a +3 bonus to Intimidate checks. A laborer typically serves an 11-hour work shift and engages in an hour of militia drills. Given the choice, most of the inhabitants of the town are nocturnal; however, the work yards and mines run 24 hours a day.

Although the citizens of Droaam are theoretically equal, centuries of tradition and bloodshed have ingrained certain habits in the population. On the streets, monsters typically defer to more powerful creatures, and most citizens have a good sense of this hierarchy. The goblin steps out of the way of the ogre, and the ogre steps aside for the troll. Frequently this breaks down to size, but there are definite exceptions to this rule. Even a troll steps out of a medusa's path.

People of the Five Nations might find the casual presence of deadly creatures to be unnerving.

Although they are relatively rare, medusas walk the streets of Graywall. A female medusa keeps her eyes closed, observing the world through her serpent mane; lacking this option, a male medusa typically ties a strip of gauze loosely across his eyes to protect others from his deadly gaze. But it is easy enough for an angry medusa to pull away the gauze, or for a harpy to raise her voice. In practice, this rarely occurs; Xorchylic has no patience for monsters that kill without reason, and the people of Droaam know better than to provoke their deadly fellow citizens. A medusa can kill with a gaze, just as a wizard in Sharn could fling a fireball into a crowd. But neither is likely to do so unless provoked.

宗教信仰

虽然在索拉•科伦之女的统治下团结在一起，卓姆的生物却有着极为多样的文化。卡萨克卓尔的美杜莎曾向灰墙派遣使节，他们过去很少和怨毒领地的提夫林或辛尔协定Znir Pact的豺狼人接触。因此，灰墙几乎没有什么宗教上的团结。许多微小的神龛分散在血石区和小灰墙区里，它们代表着不同氏族和种族的神。北方的牛头人们联合起来崇拜长角王子Horned Prince，但每个氏族都有自己恶魔霸主信仰,并相信所有其他霸主都是有缺陷的。当最后的挽歌的人面鸟妖崇拜激情与愤怒之歌——一种对愤恨的理解时，风暴歌手的人面鸟妖却崇敬风暴之歌，这是吞噬的一种体现。旅者那不定型的圣章为通过灰墙的变形怪提供了信息上的隐蔽。辛尔的豺狼人不信仰神或恶魔，而是举起成堆的石头来象征他们祖先所摧毁的偶像崇拜。

虽然每个团体都有自己的个人赞助人和信仰，但也有少数力量在全国各地受到尊重。其中最多的是阴影。阴影典型的祭司通常是美杜莎或恶鬼Oni，他们在劝诫中说阴影创造了怪物种族，赋予他的孩子们那些奥林跟其他自私的天命诸神没有给予人类的力量。到目前为止，阴影的理念在很大程度上支持了索拉•科伦的女儿们，这些祭司经常担任社区领袖。

嘲弄也受到许多种的怪物的尊敬，但他的信徒更具攻击性。嘲弄的祭司经常煽对东方人的暴力行为，在午夜的血石区遇见剥皮者Skinners（嘲弄信徒）的外国人会有祸端。

卓姆的大多数生物对天命诸神跟他们的崇拜者漠不关心。他们认为天命诸神软弱又自私，并会对追随他们的信仰感到可笑而不是冒犯。但银焰教会是另一回事。在过去的四个世纪里，银焰的拥护者和十字军经常入侵卓姆，而灰墙的人民无意原谅这些古时的入侵。任何公开佩戴银焰圣徽的人都会遭到极大的敌意。在卡拉巴斯区之外，这大大增加了遭遇街头暴力的可能性。即使他没被攻击，玩家会在交涉和市井相关的技能判定上承受-5的罚值，而任何被认为是他盟友的人也会因为这种关联而对这些检定承受-3的罚值。

Religion

Though united under the Daughters of Sora Kell, the creatures of Droaam have wildly diverse cultures. The medusas of Cazhaak Draal have sent envoys to Graywall, but in the past they have had infrequent contact with the tieflings of the Venomous Demesne or the gnolls of the Znir Pact. As a result, little religious solidarity exists in Graywall. A host of tiny shrines are scattered throughout Bloodstone and Little Graywall, and they represent the deities of different clans and races. The minotaurs of the north are united in worship of the Horned Prince, but each clan has their own representation of this demon overlord and believes all others to be flawed. The Last Dirge harpies revere the Song of Passion and Rage—an interpretation of the Fury—while the Stormsinger harpies venerate the Stormsong, an aspect of the Devourer. The asymmetric icons of the Traveler hold hidden messages for doppelgangers who pass through Graywall. The Znir gnolls worship no deity or demon, instead raising piles of stones to reflect the idols shattered by their ancestors.

Although each group has their own personal patrons and beliefs, a few forces are respected throughout the nation. The greatest of these is the Shadow. The priests of the Shadow are typically medusas or oni, and they teach that the Shadow created the monstrous races, granting his children powers that Aureon and the selfish Sovereigns withheld from humanity. So far the Voices of the Shadow have largely supported the Daughters of Sora Kell, and these priests frequently serve as community leaders.

The Mockery is also revered by monsters of many species, but his followers are more aggressive. Priests of the Mockery frequently incite violence against the people of the east, and woe betide the foreigner who encounters a pack of Skinners at midnight in Bloodstone

Most of the creatures of Droaam are indifferent toward the Sovereign Host and their worshipers. They consider the Sovereigns to be weak and selfish gods,and see their worship as more laughable than offensive. The Silver Flame is another story. Champions and crusaders of the Silver Flame have frequently invaded Droaam over the last four centuries, and the people of Graywall have no intention of forgiving these old trespasses. Anyone who openly wears a symbol of the Silver Flame is met with great hostility. Outside the Calabas, this dramatically increases the chance of street violence. Even if nobody attacks him, the PC take a –5 penalty to skill checks or challenges involving Diplomacy or Streetwise, and any perceived allies of his take a –3 penalty to these checks because of the association.

法律与秩序

卓姆不受伽利法法典和伽利法条约的约束。卓姆没有统一的司法准则；相反，法律完全掌握在索拉•科伦之女指定代表的手中。在卓姆，这个代表是艾克索切莱尔，一个古老的夺心魔。艾克索切莱尔亲自训练和指挥着劫掠者卫队，这是一支食人魔、兽人和牛头人组成的部队，由人面鸟妖、石像鬼和由索拉•麦妮亚训练的战争巨魔小队提供支援。他也可以召集辛尔协定中的豺狼人雇佣兵，以及他在萨拉释克家族内部的盟友。

归根结底，灰墙是一个边境城镇。玩家可以逃脱很多惩罚，只要他们不麻烦到艾克索切莱尔。在针对犯罪对象是外国人时尤其如此。艾克索切莱尔不承认其他国家的法律，他不会惩罚在他的城镇里寻求庇护的布鲁兰土匪。但如果有人来追捕他们，艾克索切莱尔也不会派兵进行保护.....除非那些人以某种形式对艾克索切莱尔构成威胁。严重犯罪者会被立即执行死刑。对于较轻的罪行，犯罪者会被打上烙印，然后根据罪行的程度送往角斗场一段时间。如果他活到刑满，他就可以自由离开了。据说艾克索切莱尔有时会用他的心灵异能来惩罚罪犯；有些人说他会为人植入恐惧症或精神病，但这可能只是谣言。

虽然没有成文的法律，但灰墙的人们确实有权向美杜莎地方法官提出申诉。如果地方法官认为这个案子很不重要，她就会把凝视转向原告。因此，案件的数量很少——但许多身体弱小的生物都因拥有自己的发言权而感到高兴。

艾克索切莱尔授予萨拉释克起诉在卡拉巴斯区犯下的罪行的权利。为此。萨拉释克家族的昆德兰·托恩男爵可以召集自己家族的军队，包括大量的兽人士兵；他还雇佣食人魔、石像鬼和豺狼人雇佣兵。如果犯罪行为直接威胁到卡拉巴斯区的稳定，他会采取行动。否则，他只在犯罪行为证据确凿的情况下执法，这就要求原告购买萨拉释克的调查服务。所以，正义在卡拉巴斯区的范围内是可行的，但这是有代价的。

Law and Order

Droaam stands outside the Code of Galifar and the Treaty of Galifar. There is no uniform code of justice in Droaam; instead, the law lies solely in the hands of the designated representatives of the Daughters of Sora Kell. In Droaam, this is Xorchylic, an ancient mind flayer. Xorchylic personally commands the Flayer Guard, a force of ogres, orcs, and minotaurs supplemented by harpies, gargoyles, and a squad of war trolls trained by Sora Maenya. Xorchylic can also call on the mercenary gnolls of the Znir Pact, as well his allies within House Tharashk.

At the end of the day, Graywall is a frontier town. PCs can get away with a lot, as long as they don't do anything that directly inconveniences Xorchylic. This is especially true when the crime targets a foreigner. Xorchylic doesn't recognize the laws of other lands and won't punish Brelish bandits who take sanctuary in his town. But neither will he call on his guards to protect them if someone comes looking for them . . . unless, of course, the newcomers somehow become a threat to Xorchylic. Serious crimes are punished with immediate execution. For lesser crimes, a victim is branded and then sent to the arena for a period of time based on the crime. Should he survive the duration of his sentence, he is free to go. It's said that Xorchylic sometimes uses his mental powers to punish offenders; some say he implants phobias or psychoses, but this might simply be rumor.

Although there are no written laws, the people of Graywall do have the right to bring grievances before a medusa magistrate. If the magistrate considers the case to be frivolous, she turns her gaze on the plaintiff. As a result, the caseload is light—but many of the weaker creatures take pleasure in the idea that they have a voice.

Xorchylic has granted House Tharashk the right to prosecute crimes committed in the Calabas. Baron Kundran Torrn of House Tharashk can call on the forces of his own house, including a substantial force of orc soldiers, for this purpose; he also employs ogre, gargoyle, and gnoll mercenaries. He acts if a crime directly threatens the stability of the Calabas. Otherwise, he takes action only if it can be conclusively proven that a crime has occurred, which requires the plaintiff to employ the services of Tharashk inquisitives. So justice is available within the confines of the Calabas, but it comes at a price.

经济状况

索拉•科伦的女儿们还没有铸造钱币。大多数商人愿意接受东方的黄金，尤其是在卡拉巴斯区；然而，他们也习惯于以物物交换的形式提供商品和服务。豺狼人猎人以食物和肉类进行交易。矿工们可能会提供少量的贵金属或价值低廉的粗糙宝石。另一个常见的代币是来自危险生物的牙齿。女儿们悬赏捉那些对旅行和贸易构成威胁的野生动物，并奖励那些交出被杀死动物牙齿的猎人；因此，牙齿成为了一种货币形式。一种常见的做法是在牙齿上用巨人语或地精语刻下生物的种类，但大多数商人不接受牙齿进行物物交换，除非他们个人能认出这个动物。

作为DM，将卓姆市场的物物交换系统视为放置冒险引子的机会。虽然血石区的商人可能愿意接受黄金来换区他的货物，但很可能他时手头的金币很少，无法购买冒险者的宝藏。然而，他可以提供一尊古老的达坎雕像，队伍中的历史学家认为它的价值远远超过他要交换物品的价值——但要获得它的全部价值，他们必须前往达贡！商人们可以提供通往传说中的遗迹的地图，关于达坎传说的卷轴，或者从未被发现的异变魔遗址。他们还可能拥有来自终末之战的遗物，这些遗物是从五国的士兵那里典当或者偷来的。这可以是玩家角色在地牢中无法获得的宝藏的来源，也可以为一个故事奠定基础。例如，一位寡妇希望找回一位最后目击记录在灰墙的士兵丢失的家族传家宝。因此，即使是高级角色，灰墙的市场也可能提供令人惊讶的机会。恶鬼商人可能没有足够的金币与一位典范【似乎是4E的说法，11-20级】级别的玩家交易，但它可能拥有玩家角色想要的宝藏。

卓姆的主要出口产品是拜拾克金矿。然而，人们经常来卓姆处理不那么合法的商品。强盗——包括怪物的掠夺者和布鲁兰的逃兵——在地精市场上出售他们的战利品。卓姆还是各种强效炼金术物质的来源；然而，所有这些物质都有成瘾性，并带有严重的副作用，明智的角色会避免使用它们。龙血增强了奥术的力量，但有可能使施法者从内部燃烧殆尽。勇气是一种兴奋剂，可以帮助使用者克服恐惧，在一个充满怪物的小镇上非常有用......但随着时间的推移，它会引发妄想症，最终让用户处于充满极度恐惧的紧张状态。血酒是一种强力的死灵类麻醉剂（necrotic narcotic，不是很确定）。用于赋予血酒味道和效力的浆果必须浸泡在被谋杀的有思想的生物的血液中，而饮用血酒的人会从受害者的生活中获得幻象，与药物带来的总体欣快感和倦怠感混合在一起。戒断症状包括幻觉和噩梦，经常涉及最初的受害者被谋杀的情况。在五国交易这些物质是非法的，但是外国侨民和流亡者已经对血酒上瘾，犯罪组织达斯克Daask在布鲁兰和安黛尔开设了一些隐秘的血酒酒馆。

The Economy

The Daughters of Sora Kell have yet to mint coins. Most merchants willingly accept the gold of the east, especially in the Calabas; however, they are also used to bartering for goods and services. Gnoll hunters trade food and meat. Miners might offer slivers of precious metal or rough, low-value gemstones. Another common token of value is the tooth of a dangerous animal. The Daughters offer a bounty on feral creatures that pose a threat to travel and trade, and they reward hunters for turning in the teeth of kills; as a result, teeth have become a form of currency. A common practice is to inscribe the type of creature on the tooth in either Giant or Goblin, but most merchants don't accept teeth for barter unless they personally recognize the beast.

As a DM, consider the bartering system of Droaam's markets as an opportunity to plant adventure hooks. Although a merchant in Bloodstone might be willing to accept gold for his goods, odds are high that he has little gold with which to purchase treasures from adventurers. However, he could offer an ancient Dhakaani statue that the group's historian thinks is worth considerably more than what they have to trade—although to receive its full value, they have to travel to Darguun! Merchants could offer maps leading to fabled ruins, scrolls of Dhakaani lore, or strange relics of the Daelkyr that have never been identified. They might also possess relics from the Last War, which were goods pawned by or stolen from soldiers of the Five Nations. This could be a source of treasures the PCs couldn't acquire in a dungeon, or it could set the stage for a story. For instance, a widow wants to recover a family heirloom lost by a soldier last seen in Graywall. As a result, the markets of Graywall might have surprising opportunities for even high-level characters. The oni merchant might not have the gold to deal with a paragon-level PC—but it might have treasures the PCs want.

Droaam's primary export is byeshk ore. However, people frequently come to Droaam to deal in less savory goods. Bandits—both monstrous raiders and Brelish deserters—sell their spoils in the goblin markets. Droaam is also the source of a variety of potent alchemical substances; however, all these are addictive and carry side effects severe enough that wise PCs avoid them. Dragon's blood enhances arcane powers, but carries the risk of burning out a caster from within. Courage is a stimulant that helps the user overcome fear, a useful thing in a town of monsters . . . but over time it promotes paranoia, ultimately leaving the user in a catatonic state of sheer terror. Blood gin is a powerful necrotic narcotic. The berries used to give blood gin its flavor and potency must be steeped in the blood of a murdered sentient being, and those who drink the gin have visions drawn from the life of the victim, mixed in with the overall euphoria and lassitude imparted by the drug. Withdrawal symptoms include hallucinations and nightmares, frequently involving the circumstances of the original victim's murder. Traffic in all these substances is illegal in the Five Nations, but expatriates and exiles have become addicted to blood gin, and the criminal organization Daask has opened a few hidden gin dens in Breland and Aundair.

娱乐

除了卡拉巴斯区之外，灰墙的人们主要追求简单的娱乐。许多人喜欢好好打一架，无论是在街头进行简单的摔跤比赛，酒馆里的擂台赛，还是角斗场中的大乱斗。其他形式的娱乐因种族而异。地精喜欢歌曲和音乐。灰墙的半兽化人们练习一种结合了他们的变形能力的舞蹈。食人魔喜欢力量和耐力的考验，冒险者们在血石区游荡时可能会遇到一群食人魔举起巨石的情景。虽然很少有市民拥有金币，但许多人喜欢赌博，经常可以看到牙齿或其他交换代币散落在临时比赛周围的地面上。

除了烈酒，灰墙的许多怪物还会尝试一种不寻常的麻醉品：人面鸟妖之歌。这是在谷末磨坊提供的一项公共服务，劳动者在休息时间前往谷末磨坊，大多数酒馆也会有一只"鸟歌妖songbird"。当以这种方式表演时，人面鸟妖很少会让她的声音具有完全的麻痹效果；尽管如此，她们的歌声具有催眠般的美感。鹰身女妖通常具有交涉+12和洞察+10。

Entertainment

Beyond the Calabas, the people of Graywall are largely given over to simple pleasures. Many enjoy a good brawl, whether a simple wrestling match in the street, a prize fight in a tavern, or a battle royale in the arena. Other forms of entertainment vary by species. Goblins enjoy song and music. The changelings of Graywall practice a form of dance that incorporates their shapeshifting abilities. Ogres love tests of strength and endurance, and adventurers wandering through Bloodstone might come upon a group of ogres holding massive stones over their heads. Although few citizens have gold, many love to gamble, and it's common to see teeth or other bartering tokens scattered on the ground around an impromptu match.

In addition to grog, many of the monsters of Graywall partake of an unusual intoxicant: harpy song. This is provided as a public service in the grist mills where laborers go in their off hours, and most public houses also have a "songbird." When performing in this capacity, a harpy rarely engages the fully stupefying effect of her voice; nonetheless, her songs possess a hypnotic beauty. Harpy songbirds typically possess Diplomacy +12 and Insight +10.

食物和饮料

来自东方的侨民可以在卡拉巴斯的小旅馆和小酒馆里找到熟悉的食物。如果他冒险进入血石或灰墙的其他区域，他可能会发现更令人不安的美食。尽管许多蠹灵喜欢吃还在鲜活蠕动的肉，但这些仍然总体上可以食用。其他菜肴则与类人生物的消化道不兼容。巨魔喜欢嚼干瘪的食腐虫触须，这可以使典型的人类陷入瘫痪。许多菜肴也配有微型泥怪，用于吞下去以帮助消化。然而，有坊间传言称，咀嚼不充分的泥怪会一路从用餐者的胃里吃出来。

   索拉•科伦的女儿们他们的劳工提供基本的食物，这些食物在被称为"谷末磨坊"的用餐厅供应。这个名称来源于主要的菜肴——一种被称为"谷末"的碎肉，以炖肉、派或香肠的形式准备。"谷末"口感坚韧，味道酸涩，矮人们尤其讨厌它，但它很顶饱，磨坊似乎有无穷无尽的谷末供应。很少有劳工知道谷末来自哪里，而那些知道的人中，又很少有人在意；然而，这可能会给外地人造成烦恼。每个磨坊都包括一群被囚禁的巨魔。有些是为了这个目的而饲养的，有些是因为无能而受罚的；还有一些选择为了他们的国家做出这种牺牲。肉被剥离的速度足够慢，以确保巨魔可以在没有严重伤害的情况下从损伤中再生（虽然仍会疼痛不堪）。巨魔肉含有许多轻微的毒素，通常使其无法食用。然而，索拉•科伦的女儿们掌握了解决这个困境的方法。在肉被磨碎时，混合了一种秘密的草药和香料，这可以抵消恶心的影响......尽管冒险者可能会在得知他们所吃的东西时感到反感。

Food and Drink

An expatriate from the east can find familiar foods in the inns and taverns of the Calabas. If he ventures into Bloodstone or the other districts of Graywall, he can find more disturbing fare. Most of it is perfectly edible, though many Droaamites like their meat to be squirming. Other dishes are incompatible with the human digestive tract. Trolls like to chew dried carrion crawler tendrils, which can paralyze a typical human. Many dishes are also served with a tiny ooze, which is swallowed to help digestion. However, stories have circulated that tell of poorly prepared oozes eating their way out of a diner's stomach.

The Daughters of Sora Kell provide their laborers with basic sustenance, which is served at dining halls known as "grist mills." This name is drawn from the primary dish—a form of ground meat called grist, prepared in stew, pie, or sausage form. Grist is tough and has a sour taste that dwarves find especially offensive, but it is filling and the mills have a seemingly endless supply. Few laborers have any idea where grist comes from, and of those who do, few care; however, it might disturb visitors. Grist is ground troll meat. Each mill includes a stable of imprisoned trolls. Some have been bred for this purpose; others are being punished for incompetence; and some have chosen to make this sacrifice for their nation. Flesh is stripped away, slowly enough to ensure that the troll can regenerate from the loss without serious harm (though the pain is agonizing). Troll meat contains a number of minor toxins that normally render it inedible. However, the Daughters of Sora Kell possess the answer to this dilemma. A secret blend of herbs and spices is mixed in with the meat as it is ground up, and this counteracts the nauseating effects . . . though adventurers might still be revolted when they learn what they are eating

灰墙的形状

一万年前，达坎地精建造了一座堡垒，保卫灰墙山脉和拜拾克山脉之间的山谷。即使在那时，卓姆也是一片怪物的土地，而达坎人在灰墙之后建造了许多城市，他们希望确保他们能守住这个关口。在瑟奥瑞特入侵期间，科拉什卡尔(Korash Khaar)要塞在多年的战斗中屹立不倒。然后，异变魔进入了战场。腐化者迪恩来到科拉什卡尔，堡垒在他面前倒下。有人说，堡垒的城墙吸收了守卫，这解释了剩余的石头呈血红色的原因。不管真相如何，科拉什卡尔已经不存在，山口被放弃了。

数千年来，许多不同的团体在科拉什卡尔的废墟中寻找庇护。野蛮的地精们争夺那些沾满鲜血的石头。地精们被豺狼人驱逐，而后者在博鲁南大迁徙期间又失去了这片土地，被巨魔和恶鬼取而代之。接着，巨魔们也放弃了这里，关于这段时间，传说中有红色的石像鬼从石头中出现，黑暗从城镇下方升腾而起。此后，这个闹鬼的废墟就被人们避开...直到索拉·凯尔的女儿们的到来。传说，是索拉•塔里莎发现了数千年前被束缚在科拉什卡尔下方的艾克索切莱尔。这个工程被委托给了夺心魔，他被给予了一支军队来执行任务。一些古老的的地精建筑得到了修复，而其他的则被掏空石材后重新建造。采石场迅速建立起来，食人魔们拖运着巨大的石块来满足这座城镇不断增长的需求。

THE SHAPE OF GRAYWALL

Ten thousand years ago, the Dhakaani goblins built a fortress to defend the valley between the Graywall Mountains and the Byeshk Mountains. Droaam was a land of monsters even then, and while the Dhakaani built a number of cities past the Graywall, they wanted to be sure they could hold the gate. During the Xoriat Incursion, the fortress of Korash Khaar stood strong for years of battle. Then the daelkyr took to the field of battle. Dyrrn the Corruptor came to Korash Khaar, and the fortress fell before him. Some say the fortress walls absorbed the guardians, and that this accounts for the blood-red coloring of the remaining stones.Whatever the truth of the matter, Korash Khaar was no more, and the pass was abandoned.

Over the course of millennia, many different groups took shelter in the ruins of Korash Khaar. Savage goblins fought over the bloodstained stones. The goblins were driven out by gnolls, who in turn lost the land to ogres and oni during the Borunan Exodus. The ogres abandoned it, and the tales of this time speak of ruddy gargoyles emerging from the stone and a darkness rising from beneath the town. Following this, the haunted ruins were shunned . . . until the coming of the Daughters of Sora Kell. The tales say that it was Sora Teraza who found Xorchylic, who had been bound beneath Korash Khaar thousands of years earlier. The project was entrusted to the mind flayer, and he was given an army with which to work. A number of the old goblin buildings were restored, while others were stripped for their stone and rebuilt. Quarries were quickly established, and ogres hauled great blocks of stone to feed the appetite of the growing town.

建筑和外观

灰墙是一个由巨魔的力量建造，由艾克索切莱尔和卡萨克卓尔的美杜莎石匠共同的愿景设计的城镇。大多数建筑物都是由裸露的花岗岩构成的功能性建筑。但当一个人走过某块普通的石头时，它似乎会在视觉的边缘中变成流动的涟漪——这是由艾克索切莱尔设计的奇异纹理导致的。这种潜意识的微小图形在有思想的生物之中会产生明显的情感反应。一个旅行者发现，当她走进一条小巷时会感到轻微的恐惧，而开放的广场则令人感觉愉悦和平静。地点由简单的图标来注明；灰墙的大部分居民都是文盲，只有图像才对这些蠢笨的居民有用。

灰墙建在一个隧道网络之上，许多建筑都至少有一层是地下的。其中一些地下通道一直连接到被称为凯拉斯Khyras的地区，但这些通道通常被保护或封锁；尽管如此，一群冒险家可能会找到一条通往深门的隐藏通道，或者从下方的凯拉斯闯入一座建筑。

大部分的灰墙都被设计为可以容纳各种形状和大小的怪物。门廊的拱形足够大，因此巨魔可以不用挤着身子就能通过。有些建筑为狗头人和地精设置了第二扇门以供使用；另一些建筑则在正门上开出一个小门，上面盖着一个皮革窗帘，以遮挡天气。任何类型的公共建筑都包括各种大小的凳子和椅子，以及地板上的兽皮，以供更大的生物坐在上面。公共建筑通常会有很高的天花板并在墙上设置一些栖木，供人面鸟妖和石像鬼使用。

Architecture and Appearance

Graywall is a town built with the strength of ogres, designed by the shared vision of Xorchylic and the medusa masons of Cazhaak Draal. Most buildings are functional structures of bare granite. But as one walks past the plain stone, it appears to ripple and flow in peripheral vision—the result of strange textures designed by Xorchylic. This subliminal patterning produces a noticeable emotional response in sentient beings. A traveler finds that she feels a slight touch of dread when she steps into an alley, while the open square feels pleasant and peaceful. Locations are identified by simple, iconic images; many of the inhabitants of Graywall cannot read, and only the basic icons work for the more dimwitted residents.

Graywall is built atop a network of tunnels, and many buildings have at least one level that lies underground. Some of these subterranean passages connect all the way to the region known as Khyras, but these passages are typically warded or blocked off; nonetheless, a group of adventurers might find a hidden passage to the Deep Door, or break into a building by coming up from Khyras.

Most of Graywall is designed to accommodate monsters of all shapes and sizes. The arch of a doorway is large enough for a troll to step through without squeezing. Some buildings have a second door for goblins or kobolds; others place a hatch in the main door, with a leather curtain draped down to keep out the weather. Any sort of public room includes stools and chairs in a variety of sizes, as well as skins along the floor for larger creatures to sit upon. Public buildings are likely to have high ceilings and a few perches set up along the walls—posts for harpies and gargoyles.

灰墙

这个城镇建在古代地精遗迹上，标志着卓姆和布鲁兰的边界。由地精和食人魔组成的队伍不断地工作，以扩大城镇和城墙的规模。

人口：5800人；大约有500名劳工在采石场里扎营。商人、旅行者和卓姆的军队经常经过灰墙。大部分人口是地精、兽人、豺狼人、半兽化人和食人魔，但城镇中还有相当多种的生物，包括牛头人、石像鬼、人面鸟妖、变形怪、兽化人、恶鬼Oni和美杜莎。人类、半兽人、矮人和其他种族的成员大多集中在卡拉巴斯区；而住在血石区里的人大多是强盗或逃犯

城防：劫掠者卫队The Flayer Guard既是一支维和武装力量，也是一支纪律严明、装备齐全的，卓姆最好的军队之一。除了劫掠者卫队的300名士兵之外，辛尔协定Znir Pact的豺狼人也在这里驻扎了100人的卫戍部队，而且艾克索切莱尔可以根据需求随时召集他们。尽管灰墙具有作为边境城镇的特色，但它同时也是一个军事前哨站，随时准备抵御布鲁兰的侵略....或是准备对他们发起突袭。因此，所有具有劳动力的成员都要接受基本的民兵训练，并在必要时可以拿起武器。与劫掠者卫队相比，这个民兵组织组织简陋，装备有限，但其天生的能力仍然使其成为一个不容小觑的力量。

GRAYWALL

Built atop ancient goblin ruins, this town marks the border between Droaam and Breland. Teams of goblins and ogres work ceaselessly to expand the town and its walls.

Population: 5,800; roughly 500 additional laborers are camped in the Stone Pits. Traders, travelers, and Droaamish troops pass through Graywall on a regular basis. The bulk of the population is goblins, orcs, gnolls, shifters, and ogres, but a vast array of creatures live in the town, including minotaurs, gargoyles, harpies, doppelgangers, lycanthropes, oni, and medusas. Humans, half-orcs, dwarves, and members of the other races are largely concentrated in the Calabas; those who live in Bloodstone are largely bandits or fugitives

Government: The mind flayer Xorchylic is the governor of Graywall, and it holds the power of life and death over its inhabitants. Baron Kundran Torrn of House Tharashk has been granted the right to administer justice in the Calabas.

Defense: The Flayer Guard is both a peacekeeping force and a well-disciplined and well-equipped military unit that is among the best Droaam can field. In addition to the 300 soldiers of the Flayer Guard, the gnolls of the Znir Pact keep 100 troops garrisoned in the town, and Xorchylic can call in the Pact as needed. Although it has the trappings of a border town, Graywall is also a military outpost that stands ready to defend the pass against Brelish aggression . . . or to launch a raid, if it comes to that. As such, all members of the labor force undergo basic militia training and can take up arms if necessary. In comparison to the Flayer Guard, this militia is poorly organized and has limited equipment, but the natural abilities of the creatures still make it a force with which to be reckoned.

血石 (Sorghun)

人口：血石区是灰墙最大的区，也是镇上大多数大中型居民的家园——牛头人、半兽化人、食人魔、不和萨拉释克相一致的兽人等等。血石也是变形怪、提夫林、各种土匪、逃兵和来自东边的避难罪犯的家园。

特点：这个地区的名字来源于它的建筑中采用的深红色的石头——从科拉什卡尔破碎的墙壁中挖出的石头。血石区代表了灰墙的典型形象，食人魔和地精与土匪和战犯并肩而立。对于任何一个行为不当的越界外国人来说，这都是一个危险的地方。巨魔并不一定会主动攻击冒险者，但也不会拒绝任何一场战斗，而且街头上到处都是愿意加入混战的怪物

旅店：海德拉宿舍，迷宫旅馆。

酒馆：血牙酒馆，断剑酒馆，毒声酒馆

供应商：齿与钢。

龙纹家族服务：无。

寺庙：阴影之街。

Bloodstone (Sorghun)

Population: Bloodstone is the largest district and home to most of the Medium and Large inhabitants of the town—minotaurs, shifters, ogres, orcs not aligned with House Tharashk, and others. Bloodstone is also home to doppelgangers, tieflings, and a variety of bandits, deserters, and criminals from the eastern lands who have found refuge in Graywall.

Character: The district takes its name from the crimson coloring of the stone used in many of its buildings—stones recovered from the shattered walls of Korash Khaar. Bloodstone is the classic image of Graywall, with ogres and goblins rubbing shoulders with brigands and war criminals. It is a dangerous place for any foreigner who steps out of line. A troll

doesn't necessarily pick a fight with an adventurer, but it won't turn one down, and the streets are full of monsters ready to join in a brawl.

Inns: The Hydra Halls; The Labyrinth.

Taverns: The Bloody Tooth; The Broken Sword; The Venomous Voice.

Supplies: Tooth and Steel.

Dragonmarked Services: None.

Temples: The Street of Shadows.

KEY LOCATIONS

Bloodstone is home to a variety of unsavory establishments; the following are merely a few of the more stable businesses.

角斗场：卓姆的人们热衷于他们的血腥娱乐运动，每天晚上角斗场都会发生战斗。战斗包括正式的私仇决斗，混战，与凶猛的野兽或被定罪的罪犯的赏金战，以及对劫掠者卫队士兵的测试。一种流行的赏金战形式是"六石"，七名挑战者在角斗场中，与一只或多只石化蜥蜴对决；挑战者不得与石化蜥蜴发生直接的战斗或互相杀死，比赛一直进行到只剩下一名挑战者。希望在角斗场上战斗的玩家可以寻找一名赞助人，以与其共享奖金的形式进入擂台(level 4 skill challenge, complexity 1, primary skill: Streetwise)（译注：应该是4e东西，没看懂）.。两位可能的赞助商分别是科尔·阿什塔Kel'Ashta，一个塔拉什克兽人，负责在角斗场上监视可能招募的雇佣兵；还有菲拉·骨指Fela Fingerbones,，一个聪明的地精游荡者，其家族在灰墙的赌博业中扎根。

The Arena: The people of Droaam love their blood sports, and battles take place in the arena every night. Combats include formal grudge matches, open melees, bounty battles with fierce beasts or condemned criminals, and trials for the soldiers of the Flayer Guard. One popular form of bounty battle is Six Stones, in which seven challengers are set in the arena with one or more basilisks; the combatants are not allowed to fight the basilisks or kill one another, and the contest continues until only one contestant remains. PCs who wish to fight in the arena can seek a sponsor who can get them into the ring for a share of the purse (level 4 skill challenge, complexity 1, primary skill: Streetwise). The two likely sponsors are Kel'Ashta, a Tharashk orc who monitors the arena for possible mercenary recruits; and Fela Fingerbones, a clever goblin rogue whose family has been spreading its roots into gambling activities across Graywall.

血牙酒馆：血牙是一家粗糙的小酒馆；顾客之间的任何纠纷都可以在公共休息室的挑战擂台上迅速解决。

血牙酒馆主要面向怪物顾客，人类和与他们这类的种族在这里只能受到冷淡的欢迎。

The Bloody Tooth: The Blood Tooth is a rough tavern; any disputes between patrons can be settled quickly in the challenge ring in the common room.

The Bloody Tooth caters primarily to monstrous clientele, and humans and their ilk receive a cold welcome here.

断剑酒馆：一个面向各国前士兵的酒馆，从奥法骑士到达坎的锁链大师冠军，总是有这样的人在这里。这家酒馆位于血石区，其顾客包括战犯、强盗和革命者。一个名叫莫尔的赛尔战俑既做酒保又做保镖；也许他就是老板，不然就是真正的老板选择保持匿名。断剑酒馆的顾客被期望把他们的国家纷争丢到酒馆外面。在断剑酒馆内，瑟雷恩圣武士和坎纳斯骸骨骑士都是流亡者。这里的潜在规矩是，如果有人在断剑酒馆内引发争端，尤其是哨兵执法官或其他赏金猎人，每位顾客都会伸出援手，采取行动以尽快结束争斗。

The Broken Sword: A tavern catering to ex-soldiers of the eastern nations, the Broken Sword has Knights Arcane to Darguul chainmasters within it at any given moment. Located in Bloodstone, this tavern counts war criminals, brigands, and revolutionaries among its clients. A Cyran warforged named Maul serves as both bartender and bouncer; either he owns the tavern, or the true owner has chosen to remain anonymous. Patrons of the Broken Sword are expected to leave their national feuds outside. Within the Broken Sword, the Thrane paladin and Karrnathi bone knight are both exiles. The unwritten rule is that if anyone starts a fight in the Sword—especially a Sentinel Marshal or other bounty hunter—every patron lends a hand, acting to end the fight as quickly as possible.

挑战之环：角斗场是进行宏伟战斗的场所，但灰墙的任何地方都不会缺少冲突。在血石区的许多主要路口都存在刻在地上的圆环。大多数内都含有大块石头、破碎的雕像的碎片或干燥的血迹。在这些环中会发生各种各样的决斗。搏斗和力量的壮举非常普遍。牛头人会进行一种摔跤，两名战斗者试图将对手推出或撞出环外。大多数这类挑战都涉及到运动、耐力或技巧的技能挑战，但也有其他选择。狗头人的决斗是一场以侮辱和贬低为主的对战，由旁观者对结果进行评判；这样的挑战可以使用洞察、恫吓和虚张声势来得到成功。

Challenge Rings: The arena is the sight of grand battles, but no shortage of conflict exists throughout Graywall. Circles are scratched into the ground at many of the major intersections in Bloodstone. Most contain large chunks of stone, shards of broken statues, or patches of dried blood. A wide variety of challenges take place in these rings. Brawling and feats of strength are commonplace. Minotaurs engage in a form of wrestling in which the two combatants seek to push or shove the opponent out of the ring. Most of these are skill challenges involving Athletics, Endurance, or Acrobatics, but there are alternatives. A kobold duel is a match of insults and put-downs, judged by the onlookers; such a challenge could use Insight, Intimidate, and Bluff to determine success.

蠕虫之家：一个受伤的角斗士如果承受不起乔拉斯科家族的服务，他可以在血石区的蠕虫之家碰碰运气。经营这家旅馆的是一名头发花白，脾气古怪的长牙半兽化人，他被称为蠕虫，因为他使用孵出的蠕虫治疗受感染的伤口。虽然他的技术令人不安，但蠕虫有+15的医疗技能。（译注：这是4e的数值）

通常蠕虫只能提供平凡的治疗，但他总是在寻找仪式卷轴，在任何时候，他都可能用一个仪式进行协助。

The House of Worms: An injured gladiator who can't afford the services of House Jorasco can try his luck at the House of Worms in Bloodstone. The grizzled and ornery longtooth shifter who runs the hostel is known as Worms, due to his use of broodworms in treating infected wounds. Although his techniques are disturbing, Worms has a Heal skill of +15.

Usually Worms can provide only mundane treatment, but he's always on the lookout for ritual scrolls, and at any given time he might assist with a ritual.

海德拉宿舍：灰墙是一个为容纳成千上万人而建造的年轻城镇。海德拉宿舍是大规模的宿舍群，每个宿舍在布局和外观上几乎与其他宿舍相同。宿舍唯一的独特特征是在主入口上方的独特图标。因此，一个食人魔知道他生活在"眼"重还是"爪"中。大多数宿舍都被分配给了特定的工作人员，但也有一些房间可供怪物旅行者以物物交换的形式使用。大厅分布在两个巨大的谷末磨坊周围，工人们去那里得到食物，饮料，还有人面鸟妖的歌声。

The Hydra Halls: Graywall is a young town that was built to house thousands. The Hydra Halls are mass dormitories, and each is virtually identical in layout and appearance to the others. The only distinctive feature of a hall is the unique icon painted above the main entrance. As such, an ogre knows that he lives in the Eye or the Claw. Most of the halls are assigned to specific work crews, but a few have rooms available for monstrous travelers on a barter basis. The halls are spread around the two enormous grist mills, where the laborers go for food, drink, and harpy song.

贾巴拉的店：作为索拉·科伦的女儿的远亲，夜巫婆贾巴拉是一个技艺非凡的炼金术士。除了销售传统的药水，她还生产龙血、血酒和其他不寻常但可能很危险的灵药。她还会把梦和噩梦装瓶，以足够的价格，她可以为每个玩家角色获得特定个体的梦境。贾巴拉的店面是一个简单的帐篷，她每天都在血石周围走动，但有时会消失几天或几周。

Jabra's: A distant relative of the Daughters of Sora Kell, the night hag Jabra is an alchemist of remarkable skill. In addition to selling traditional potions, she produces dragon's blood, blood gin, and other unusual and possibly dangerous elixirs. She also bottles dreams and nightmares, and for a sufficient price she could acquire the dreams of a specific individual for the PCs. Jabra's storefront is a simple tent, and she moves around Bloodstone on a daily basis, sometimes disappearing for days or weeks.

迷宫旅店：迷宫旅馆实际上是一个在建在老采石场地面的洞。其房间散落在迷宫般的通道中，使它成为一个不想被发现的逃犯的完美的家。一群牛头人拥有这家旅馆，他们认真对待客人的隐私。每晚房间的平均费用为2金币，长期租贷可打九折。考虑到住宿条件的简朴性质，这个价格偏高，但迷宫的目标对象是那些有金币且需要隐私的逃亡者。

The Labyrinth: Built at the base of an old quarry, the Labyrinth is a literal hole in the ground. Its rooms are scatteredthroughout a maze of passages, making it the perfect home away from home for the fugitive who doesn't want to be found. A family of minotaurs owns the bar, and they take the privacy of their guests seriously. The average cost of a room is 2 gp per night, with a 10% discount for long-term rental. The price is high given the austere nature of the accommodations, but the Labyrinth caters to fugitives who have gold and need privacy.

皮肤工厂：尽管人们可以在磨坊吃一顿饭，但没有人喜欢磨坊。皮肤工厂是指位于血石区边缘的屠宰场、屠夫和制革工坊的集合。皮肤工厂的商人正在寻找新鲜的肉，将任何猎物带到该地区的玩家角色可以在交易中得到更有用的东西，无论是贵金属块还是保存完好的路上口粮。皮肤工厂包括霍拉克的皮革，这是一个兽人制革家族，他们制作出色的皮革和兽皮护甲。尽管他们的大部分工作是平凡的，但祖父盖奇Gech从恶鬼那里学到了一些诀窍，可以制作出魔法盔甲。赢得霍拉克家族青睐的玩家角色可能会得到由盖奇设计的独特护甲。

The Skin Factory: Although laborers can eat a meal at a grist factory, no one likes grist. The Skin Factory is the name given to the collection of slaughterhouses, butchers, and tanners found on the edge of Bloodstone. The merchants of the Skin Factory are looking for fresh meat, and PCs who bring any kill to the district can get something more useful in trade, whether nuggets of precious metal or preserved rations for the road. The Skin Factory includes Horac's Hides, which is home to a family of orc tanners who make excellent leather and hide armor. Although most of their work is mundane, grandfather Gech learned tricks from an oni and can produce enchanted armor. A PC who earns the favor of the Horacs might be offered a unique piece designed by Gech.

阴影之街：在灰墙，各处都散布着朝拜恶魔和黑暗六神的神龛，但影之街上则是它们的聚集之地。从辛尔豺狼人的石堆到牛头人的有角神像，这里有数十个圣像和祭坛。街道的尽头是阴影之眼，一个由黑色石头构建的小型无窗寺庙。这里是美杜莎女祭司泽拉莎的法庭。作为一名可怕的战士和熟练的仪式施法者，泽拉莎是继艾克索切莱尔之后灰墙中最有影响力的声音；城镇的人们开始信任她的预言天赋。目前，她是索拉·科尔的女儿们的盟友，但她首要的效忠对象是阴影和她的军阀，即石之女王（译注：在kb的某篇没翻译的小说里）。如果灰墙爆发内战，这位黑鳞美杜莎将成为一股不可忽视的力量。

The Street of Shadows: Shrines to demons and various faces of the Dark Six are scattered across Graywall, but the Street of Shadows is lined with them. From the cairns of the Znir gnolls to the horned idols of the minotaurs, there are dozens of icons and altars. The street ends at the Eye of the Shadow, a small windowless temple formed from black stone. The medusa priestess Zerasha holds court here. A fearsome combatant and skilled ritual caster, Zerasha is the most influential voice in Graywall after Xorchylic; the people of the town have come to trust her oracular gifts. At the moment, she is an ally of the Daughters of Sora Kell, but her first loyalty is to the Shadow and to her own warlord, the Queen of Stone. Should there ever be a civil war in Graywall, the black-scaled medusa will be a force with which to be reckoned.

齿和钢：劫掠者卫队的成员由卡达区的铁匠铺和小灰墙的地精提供装备，但卓姆总会有人需要武器和盔甲。由半兽化人和牛头人主要组成的齿与钢铁匠铺生产出各种尺寸的高度独特（尽管非魔法）的装备。锻造大师是恶鬼法师Szalas Jal，他将神秘研究集中在附魔武器的制造上。Szalas花费数年时间在他的武器上，它们是强大的工具；他很少卖这些杰作，但可能会有例外

Tooth and Steel: Members of the Flayer Guard are outfitted by the Karda Forge and the goblins of Little Graywall, but there are always customers for arms and armor in Droaam. Staffed largely by shifters and minotaurs, Tooth and Steel produces highly distinctive (albeit mundane) equipment in all sizes. The master of the forge is the oni mage Szalas Jal, who has focused his mystical studies on the creation of enchanted weaponry. Szalas spends years working on his weapons, and they are tools of great power; he rarely sells these masterpieces, but might make an exception.

毒声酒馆：血石的所有磨坊和大多数酒馆都有一只人面鸟妖常驻，但它们通常是在磨练技艺的新手。这些致命歌手中最优秀的部分都在毒声酒馆，这是一家为灰墙的独立鸟妖提供栖息的住所，也是勇敢的顾客聆听鸟妖大师之间音乐对决的场地。毒声酒馆的娱乐项目还包括讲述故事、合唱训练（用于教授年轻哈比基本技能）以及最有天赋的塞壬的专场表演。许多参观过毒声的人会冒着前往血石的风险来重返酒馆，一些厌倦了普通的游客甚至会专程从五国而来，只为聆听传奇的表演。毒声酒馆由最后的挽歌航班（译注：没看懂）的Zoryanna经营，她是愤恨的虔诚信徒。"

The Venomous Voice: All the grist mills and most of the taverns of Bloodstone have a harpy songbird in residence, but these are frequently novices honing their skills. The best of these deadly singers reside at the Venomous Voice, a tavern that serves both as a roost for the independent harpies of Graywall and a place for daring patrons to hear musical duels between harpy virtuosos. Entertainment at the Voice also includes storytelling, choral training (used to teach the youngest harpies the basic skills), and command performances by the most gifted sirens. Many who visit the Voice find themselves braving the dangers of Bloodstone to return to the harpy tavern, and some jaded tourists travel from the Five Nations to hear the legendary performances. The Venomous Voice is managed by Zoryanna of the Last Dirge flight, a devotee of the Fury.

冒险引子

✦ PC的盟友被判处在角斗场中战斗一周，而他能够在那么长时间内生存下来的几率并不高。PC能否找到一种方法在比赛之间将他从角斗场中解救出来？或者他们会在角斗场中与他并肩作战？

✦ 齿与钢的Szalas Jal打造了一把刻有龙印纹样的刀剑，他相信这把刀剑在预言中有一定的角色。他认为PC中的某人可能注定要挥舞这把武器。但要证明这一点，PC必须完成一个较小的预言——穿过深门，战胜藏身于城镇下的秽染公子。

✦ PC获得了一个瓶装梦境，暗示着有人正在策划一些他们想要阻止的事情（从杀死PC之一到在卓姆和之间发动战争等）。他们能找到贾布拉吗？如果能的话，他们能说服她告诉他们从谁那里收获了这个梦境的人的身份吗？

The Calabas (The Kennel)

Population: House Tharashk is the pillar of this district, and humans, orcs, and half-orcs make up a significant segment of the population. Most of the expatriates and travelers from the east reside in the Calabas or at least pass through it, and most of the elves, dwarves, gnomes, and halflings of Graywall can be found here. Many menial jobs are held by goblins, though few live in the district.

Character: Surprisingly civilized, the Calabas is a piece of the Five Nations trapped in Droaam. Buildings reflect the styles of the Five Nations. Bright colors on the buildings and in the glass windows, with painted signs bearing names written in the Common tongue, provide a flamboyant range of shades to the area. Orc guards in Tharashk livery patrol the streets, but the more frightening monsters—trolls, harpies, medusas—are rarely seen here.

KEY LOCATIONS

The Calabas contains locations that might be of interest to PCs. The numbers are keyed to the map associated with this district.

1.咆哮广场：卡拉巴斯区的中心是一个巨大的广场。它的名字来源于其中央的雕像，其样式是一只狂暴的龙狮仰天长啸。这野兽是萨拉释克家族的标志，反映了他们在这个区域的力量。除了其景观价值外，咆哮广场在夜晚是地精市场的举办地。各种各样的怪物商人前来向旅行者和游客出售他们的商品。来自血石和小灰墙的铁匠与工匠带来他们的日常商品，但敏锐的购物者可能会发现被偷的货物、从达坎中找回的文物、从阵亡士兵身上剥取的宝藏，或者其他许多令人惊喜的东西。

1. The Roar: The heart of the Calabas is a vast plaza. It takes its name from the statue at its center, which is of a rampant dragonne roaring at the sky. The beast is the sigil of House Tharashk and reflects their power in this district. Aside from its scenic value, at night the Roar plays host to the Goblin Market. All manner of monstrous merchants come to sell their wares to travelers and tourists. Smiths and artisans from Bloodstone and Little Graywall bring their mundane goods, but astute shoppers might find stolen goods, relics recovered from Dhakaani ruins, treasures stripped from fallen soldiers, or any number of other surprises.

2.萨拉释克领地：萨拉释克三巨头认卓姆能为家族提供有巨大的机遇，而他们在灰墙的领地是泽拉拾克之外最大的。除了售卖探寻者、追踪者和向导的服务外，该领地还有用于培训受雇的怪物佣兵的训练设施，并作为该地区勘探行动的中心。萨拉释克家族在卡拉巴斯区内执行法律，这里有一个会见室，巴伦·昆德兰·托恩或他的代言人可以在那里主持法庭。

2. House Tharashk Enclave: The Tharashk triumvirate believes that Droaam holds tremendous opportunity for their house, and their Graywall enclave is the largest outside Zarash'ak. In addition to selling the services of inquisitives, trackers, and guides, the enclave holds training facilities for the monstrous mercenaries the house employs and serves as a hub for prospecting operations within the region. House Tharashk enforces the law within the Calabas, and the enclave includes an audience chamber where Baron Kundran Torrn or one of his ministers can hold court.

3.暮光宫：作为费奥兰家族和伽兰达家族的合作项目，暮光宫在如此荒蛮的土地是一件奇妙的事情。暮光宫是一间豪华的旅社，提供奢华的住宿和精通科瓦雷各国风俗的礼貌员工，其设计旨在满足富裕的外籍居民或成功的商人的需需求。在其墙内，游客可以轻松地忘记她身处卓姆。费奥兰吟游诗人演奏来自伽利法黄金时代的作品，而伽兰达的厨师则制作任何老饕都会食指大动美味佳肴。由于昆达拉克提供的安全保障，宫殿像艾克索切莱尔的堡垒一样安全。当然，这样的奢华是要付出沉重代价的：住宿和膳食的价格从每晚35银币起，最高有每晚10金币的大套房。如果顾客按周租用，费用将减少20%。

3. The Twilight Palace: A joint venture of House Phiarlan and House Ghallanda, the Twilight Palace is a wondrous thing to find in such a savage land. Designed to serve the needs of the wealthy expatriate or the successful merchant, the palace is a grand hostel, with luxurious accommodations and courteous staff well-versed in the customs of every nation in Khorvaire. Within its walls, a visitor can easily forget she is in Droaam. Phiarlan minstrels perform works from the golden age of Galifar, and the Ghallanda chefs produce culinary delights to stir even jaded appetites. Thanks to Kundarak security, the Palace is as safe as Xorchylic's fortress. Of course, such luxury comes at a heavy price: room and board starts at 35 sp per night and can go as high as 10 gp per night for a grand suite. If a customer rents an apartment by the week, the cost is reduced by 20%.

4.金龙旅店：这家旅店与遍布科瓦雷各地的金龙旅店几乎相同；它是乔拉斯科家族为预算有限的旅行者提供的普遍住宿。菜单上供应着五国的简易菜肴，因此一个坎纳斯商人可以尽情享受一碗夜木炖菜，而一位瑟雷恩人则可以品尝到火烤的三角铃tribex。金龙旅店由瑞斯卡尔·D·乔拉斯科 Reskal d'Jorasco管理，他曾是一名爪足鞭手clawfoot wrangler（好像是种恐龙相关职业），身上的伤疤为他徒手捕捉恐龙的故事提供了证明。在金龙客栈住宿和膳食的费用为每晚5银币，对于连续住宿5天或更长时间的客人可以打九折。

4. The Gold Dragon Inn: This inn is virtually identical to the Gold Dragon Inns found in cities across Khorvaire; it is House Ghallanda's ubiquitous accommodations for the budget traveler. The menu serves one simple, comforting dish from each of the Five Nations, so a Karrn trader can relax with a bowl of Nightwood stew and a Thrane can enjoy flamegrilled tribex. The Gold Dragon Inn is managed by Reskal d'Jorasco, a former clawfoot wrangler with the scars to back up his stories of capturing dinosaurs barehanded. Room and board at the Gold Dragon Inn costs 5 sp per night, which is reduced by 10% for stays of 5 days or more.

5. 欢乐行者：隐蔽在咆哮广场的边缘，欢乐行者是为那些想要体验比金龙客栈更有些异域风情，但又不想冒血石区风险的旅行者提供服务的友好酒馆。欢乐行者由一位名叫杜拉德·塔什Durad Taash的半兽人拥有，并专注于阴影湿地的烹饪。来自其他地方半兽人和兽人特别受欢迎，并会被鼓励他们讲述他们的旅行故事

5. The Merry Marcher: Tucked along the edge of the Roar, the Merry Marcher is a friendly tavern for travelers who want something a little more exotic than the Gold Dragon Inn but don't want to brave the dangers of Bloodstone. The Marcher is owned by a half-orc named Durad Taash, and specializes in the cuisine of the Shadow Marches. Half-orcs and orcs from other lands are especially welcome and encouraged to tell tales of their travels.

6.沃加的店：作为一位从探矿者转行为商人的兽人，沃加向旅行者提供日常商品。沃加愿意从探矿者那里购买有趣的小玩意，他可能会从玩家角色那里购买商品或出售魔法物品；他通常手头上有1500金币。

6. Vorgath's: A former prospector turned entrepreneur, the orc Vorgath provides mundane goods to travelers. Vorgath is willing to buy interesting trinkets from prospectors, and he might buy goods from PCs or have a magic item to sell; he typically has 1,500 gp on hand.

7.昆达拉克金库：一个带有加固墙壁的小建筑，该金库由昆达拉克家族和西维斯家族共享。在灰墙，两者都没有特别强大的存在，但有一个通讯石和昆达拉克金库网络的接入点，允许该银行的客户提取存放在科瓦雷其他地方的货物。

7. The Kundarak Vault: A small building with reinforced walls, the vault is shared by House Kundarak and House Sivis. Neither has an especially strong presence in Graywall, but there is a speaking stone and an access point for the Kundarak vault network, which allows clients of the bank to withdraw goods stored elsewhere in Khorvaire.

8.法尔·达万迪（Far Davandi）：著名的吉拉哥裁缝的前哨，法尔·达万迪可以制作任何五国风格的服装。它主要为那些依然怀念家乡的外籍居民提供服务。

8. Far Davandi: An outpost of the famed Zil haberdasher, Far Davandi can produce clothing designed in the style of any of the Five Nations. It primarily serves expatriates who still remember their homeland fondly.

9.欧瑞恩家族领地：这个大型前哨站设计用于同时支持多个商队，目前他的运行能力低于设计期望。索拉·科伦的女儿们有意在她们的领土内建立贸易路线，并最终引入闪电烈车轨道，她们为欧瑞恩卓姆的运营提供了资助。当地的男爵，萨瑞亚·D·欧瑞恩Saria d'Orien，是一位狡猾的谈判者，并在镇内建立了紧密的联系。

9. House Orien Enclave: This large outpost is designed to support multiple caravans at once, and it is currently operating below capacity. The Daughters of Sora Kell are interested in establishing trade routes and eventuallylightning rails within their realm, and they have subsidized Orien's operations in Droaam. The local Baron, Saria d'Orien, is a canny negotiator and has formed close ties within the town.

10.哈洛的小屋：尽管规模较小，这个乔拉斯科治疗之屋可以处理大多数问题。由于他的龙纹，哈洛·D·乔拉斯科可以执行治疗疾病、死者复活或移除苦痛的仪式——除非他喜欢客户，否则他会在价格上加收20%的"卓姆税"。

10. Hahlo's House: Although small, this Jorasco house of healing can handle most problems. Thanks to his dragonmark, Hahlo d'Jorasco can perform Cure Disease, Raise Dead, or Remove Affliction rituals—although he adds a 20% "Droaam tax" to his prices unless he likes the clients.

11.沉默舞台：由费奥兰家族资助，这个剧院专门演出在五国被封禁的作品，以及那些流亡和被驱逐艺术家的新作。它是灰墙的主要旅游吸引力来源之一，因为人们从费尔海文、银焰堡还有寇斯前来观看这些被禁演出。该剧团的主要成员包括拉伦·德维尔Laren Devir，一位以反君主制度言辞著称的布鲁兰剧作家；半精灵阿斯塔·布兰德Asta Brand，一位坎纳斯歌手兼词曲作者，他的歌剧《亡者军团》痛斥了坎埃斯法典和坎纳斯对亡灵士兵的使用；以及赛罗·塔兰德罗Syro Talandro，来自瑟雷恩的半变形怪，以其个人表演和对银焰教堂黑暗面的探索而闻名。阿斯塔·布兰德是该公司的创始人，在被翡翠利爪骑士团变成吸血鬼后逃亡到了卓姆；她视自己为怪物，因此认为这里是她最好的去处。如果玩家角色中有艺术天赋的话，阿斯塔可能会寻求雇佣他们在沉默舞台上表演；否则，阿斯塔或其他艺术家可能会想与与她同出生地的玩家角色交谈，以寻找新作品的灵感。

11. The Silenced Stage: Subsidized by House Phiarlan, this playhouse specializes in works that have been banned in the Five Nations, along with new creations of exiled and expatriate artists. It is one of the major tourist draws in Graywall, since people travel from Fairhaven, Flamekeep, and Korth to see these forbidden performances. The luminaries of the company include Laren Devir, a Brelish playwright known for his antimonarchy rhetoric; half-elf Asta Brand, a Karrnathi singer-songwriter whose opera "Army of the Damned" is a bitter condemnation of both the Code of Kaius and the Karrnathi use of undead soldiers; and Syro Talandro, a changeling from Thrane renowned for his solo performances and his explorations of the dark side of the Church of the Silver Flame. Asta Brand is the founder of the company, having fled to Droaam after being turned into a vampire by the Order of the Emerald Claw; she considers herself to be a monster, and as such she believes there is no better place for her. If any of the PCs have artistic talent, Asta might seek to employ them in a performance at the Silent Stage; otherwise, Asta or any of the artists might want to talk to PCs who share her birthplace to find inspiration for new works.

12.面纱：虽然规模不大，但这家妓院拥有一批变形怪，经过训练可以模仿任何五国通用的口音和举止方式。除了传统的服务外，面纱还提供经过训练的陪同人员，能够从客户的脑海中提取形象——变形怪可以模拟出一个被放逐或在战争中被杀的亲人的形象和举止，让外籍居民或旅行商人能够在失散的人陪伴下享受一顿饭或一个夜晚。

12. Veils: Though small, this brothel has a stable of doppelgangers trained to mimic the accents and mannerisms common to any of the Five Nations. In addition to traditional services, Veils also provides escorts trained to pluck an image from the mind of a client—doppelgangers who can assume the form and mannerisms of a loved one, allowing an expatriate or traveling merchant to enjoy a meal or an evening in the company of someone lost through exile or slain in the war.

13. 破裂酒桶：这家破烂的酒馆有烟熏玻璃窗户，唯一的标记是挂在门上的桶形招牌，这个没有任何标记的招牌上只有一道裂缝穿过。老板是班诺·黑桶Banor Blackbarrel，一位曾经贩卖强效的巨魔烈酒给西部边境驻军的老矮人强盗。除了提供强大的卓姆烈酒和赌博赛事外，班诺在他的地下室里经营着一个血酒窝点——对于越来越多放荡的流亡者来说，这是一个远离家乡的家。班诺在整个城镇有着广泛的关系，如果玩家角色想在血石区找到某人或某物，他可能是一个有价值的盟友。但他是一个无情而残酷的人，只关心个人利益。

13. The Cracked Keg: This seedy tavern has smoked windows, and it is identified only by the barrel-shaped trade sign hanging over the door. This sign has a single crack running through the unmarked sign. The owner is Banor Blackbarrel, an old dwarf brigand who used to run potent troll grog to the troops stationed along the western border. In addition to serving powerful Droaamish spirits and sponsoring gambling, Banor runs a blood gin den in his basement—a home away from home for a growing number of dissolute exiles. Banor has contacts throughout the town, and PCs looking to find someone or something in Bloodstone could find him to be a valuable ally. But he's a ruthless and cruel person, interested solely in his personal profit.

14.九之宅：这家小型客栈和酒馆同时也是卓姆唯一的天命诸神庙宇，由一家来自布雷兰西部的人类家庭经营。女主人查娜·佩林Channa Perryn在欧拉卓和博德雷的名义下祝福每一顿饭，而科德·佩林Cord Perryn则在后房举行仪式。科德和查娜都不是施法者，但他们为天命诸神的信徒提供心灵上的引导，科德可能会利用他的治疗技能（+14）（译注：4e数值）来协助客人。在九之宅的住宿和膳食费用为5银币，但旅馆内只有四间房。

14. The House of the Nine: This small inn and public house is also Droaam's sole temple to the Sovereign Host, run by a human family from western Breland. Hostess Channa Perryn blesses every meal in the name of Olladra and Boldrei, and Cord Perryn performs services in the back room. Neither Cord nor Channa are spellcasters, but they provide spiritual guidance to followers of the Host, and Cord might assist guests with his Heal (+14) skill. Room and board at the House of the Nine costs 5 sp, but there are only four rooms in the inn.

Population: Goblins and kobolds live here, along with a handful of gnomes and halflings scattered throughout the quarter.

Character: Sar Kuraath lives up to its name—it truly is little Graywall. Much of the town is designed to accommodate Large creatures, and the Hydra Halls have high ceilings and rooms with bunks large enough for trolls andogres. The goblin artisans produce goods for the Flayer Guard and the rest of the town, and workhouses have high ceilings and doors allowing ogres easy access to the goods they haul between districts. The goblins and kobolds of Graywall are proud of the measure of independence they have gained under the rule of the Daughters of Sora Kell, and Little Graywall is the symbol of this. Although there are tribal rivalries, goblins of different tribes almost always unite to fight a larger foe. As a result, the natives of this district are unusually bold here in their home, and they are less likely to put up with abuse from outsiders.

部落地区：每个地精或狗头人部落都有自己的建筑，这是一个既充当工作间又当做营房的结构。门上绘制的符号或墙上嵌入的符号表示居住在其中的部落：骨鸦（Bone Crows）、锤砸者（Hammer-Knockers）、隐手（Hidden Hands）、石碎者（Stonebreakers）等。总体而言，每个部落专注于特定的行业；石碎者在矿山和采石场工作，而锤砸者是铁匠和铸甲匠。

Tribal Warrens: Each goblin or kobold tribe has its own building, which is a structure that serves both as workhouse and barracks. Symbols painted over the door or embedded in the walls indicate the tribe that lives within: the Bone Crows, Hammer-Knockers, Hidden Hands, Stonebreakers, and others. By and large, each tribe focuses on a specific trade; the Stone breakers work in the mines and quarries, while the Hammer-Knockers are blacksmiths and armorers.

布莱克·巴希里的店：小灰墙中有一座磨坊，大多数居民在那里用餐或在他们的居住区准备食物。然而，有一家客栈在整个灰墙赢得了一定的声誉。布莱克·巴希里以地精的标准来说非常肥胖；他还是一位在卓姆和塔兰塔烹饪方面都很有技巧的厨师。由于他与血石区的皮革工厂进行的私下交易，他得到了一流的原材料，他的塔兰塔料理可能是东方人在暮光宫殿外找得到的最可口的食物。巴希里有出租的房间，有一些流亡的侏儒和半身人在这里定居。有人认为布莱克·巴希里是阴谋的中心；有传言说自称为Shiv的侏儒是真知社的高级特工，而巴希里是沉默之刃Shaarat'khesh的一名高级间谍。这些事情可能是真的，也可能纯属幻想。但如果一个人想要在地精歌声和愉快的伙伴陪伴下享受一顿美味的饭菜，布莱克·巴希里是一个不错的选择。巴希里确实备有供外国人食用的血酒，但他不会向地精出售这种毒品。在布莱克·巴希里租房间的费用是每晚1金币；由于他的房间数量有限，巴希里可能会拒绝一个令人不愉快的客户。

Black Bahiri's: A single grist mill is in the Little Graywall, and most of the inhabitants dine there or make meals within their warrens. However, one inn has gained a measure of renown throughout Graywall. Black Bahiri is remarkably fat for a goblin; he is also a talented chef skilled in both Droaamish and Talentan cuisines. Thanks to under-the-table deals he gets prime materials from the Skin Factory in Bloodstone, and his Talentan preparations might be the most palatable food an easterner can find outside the Twilight Palace. Bahiri has rooms for rent, and there are a few gnome and halfling exiles who make their homes here. Some believe that Black Bahiri's is a center for intrigue; there are rumors that the gnome who calls herself Shiv is a top agent of the Trust, and that Bahiri is a master spy from the Shaarat'khesh. These things might be true, or they might be pure fantasy. But if one is looking for a good meal accompanied by goblin song and cheerful company, Black Bahiri's is a fine choice. Bahiri does keep blood gin on hand for foreigners, but he does not sell the drug to goblins. Renting a room at Black Bahiri's costs 1 gp per night; since he has a limited number of rooms, Bahiri might turn down an unpleasant customer.

快速死亡：许多人来到卓姆是为了寻找在五国无法合法购买的商品......比如毒药。在地精市场可能会找到简单的毒素，但灰墙有一位真正的毒药大师，一位名叫斯威夫特（Swift）的地精。有人说他是沉默之刃Shaarat'khesh的一部分，而另一些人则声称斯威夫特通过毒死虐待过他部落的食人魔来练习手艺。安排与斯威夫特的会面需要进行技能挑战（等级7；复杂度1，主要技能为街头智谋；地精获得+3的奖励，任何在布莱克·巴希里询问的人获得+1的奖励）。尽管由DM决定斯威夫特在特定时间都有什么供应，但他是西部科瑞维尔最好的毒药来源之一。

Swift Death: Many people come to Droaam in search of goods they cannot legally buy in the Five Nations . . . such as poison. It's possible to find simple toxins in the Goblin Market, but Graywall is home to a true master poisoner, a goblin named Swift. Some say that he is part of the Shaarat'khesh, while others claim Swift learned his trade poisoning ogres who abused his tribe. Arranging a meeting with Swift requires a skill challenge (level 7; complexity 1, primary skill Streetwise; a goblin gets a +3 bonus, and anyone asking in Black Bahiri's gets a +1 bonus). It's up to the DM to decide just what Swift has available at any given time, but he's one of the best sources of poison in western Khorvaire.

冒险引子：

✦ 一位盟友中了一种对所有魔法治疗都具有抵抗力的毒素......一种原理上类似于疾病的缓慢作用毒液。如果有人能创造出一种治疗方法，那一定是斯威夫特——但玩家首先必须了解到这位=地精的存在，找到他，然后说服他帮助他们。

✦ 一位名叫乔尔Jorl的石碎者地精走近玩家。他说他的部落在深处发现了一笔巨大的财富，并且他们可以把它给予玩家——如果玩家能够谋杀一位名叫恶鬼法师和他的食人魔追随者，他们曾经奴役过石碎者。

✦ 萨恩的黑帮战争升级了，主要是由于波罗马犯罪家族的半身人扎罗Zharo的叛变。扎罗偷走了大量波罗马的财宝并将其藏起来；除非他们能够找回他们的宝藏，否则高塔之城的权力平衡将无法挽回。扎罗隐藏在布莱克·巴希里的店，喝着血酒，躲避着世界。玩家能找到他吗？他们能说服他透露宝藏的位置吗？

ADVENTURE HOOKS

✦ An ally has been poisoned by a toxin that resists all magical treatment . . . a slow-acting venom mechanically similar to a disease. If anyone can create a cure, it's Swift—but the PCs first have to learn about the goblin's existence, locate him, and then convince him to help them.

✦ A Stonebreaker goblin named Jorl approaches the PCs. He says that his clan found a great treasure in the depths, and that they can give it to the PCs—if the PCs murder a visiting oni mage and his ogre followers, who once held the Stonebreakers in bondage.

✦ The gang wars in Sharn have escalated, due largely to the treachery of Zharo, a halfling from the Boromar crime family. Zharo has stolen a lot of the Boromar's treasury and hidden it; unless they can recover their treasure, the balance of power in the City of Towers will be irrevocably altered. Zharo is hidden in Black Bahiri's, drinking blood gin and hiding from the world. Can the PCs find him, and can they convince him to reveal the location of the treasure?

卡尔达（The Throne）

人口：卡尔达是劫掠者者卫队和辛尔协定兵营的所在地。兽人、食人魔、牛头人和豺狼人构成了人口的大部分，而战争巨魔的部队则形成了一个令人印象深刻的（虽然很小）部分。除了士兵外，地精和兽人可以在这里执行琐碎的任务和平民的工作。外国人在这里并不受欢迎，很少看到人类或其他东方人。

特点：尽管它可以被视为贸易中心，但灰墙实际上是位于卓姆和布鲁兰边境的军事前哨站，而卡尔达则是城镇中心的堡垒。高大而厚重的城墙环绕着该区。步兵在街上巡逻，鸟妖和石像鬼在头顶盘旋。城门是敞开的，但牛头人哨兵会询问任何希望进入的人，要求知道他们前往卡尔达的目的。外国人要承受怀疑的对待，可能会被巡逻队审问。

客栈：无。

酒馆：无。

供应商：无。

龙印服务：伽兰达；乔拉斯科；昆达拉克；欧瑞安；费奥兰；西维斯；萨拉释克

寺庙：没有公共寺庙，但在兵营里有神龛。

The Karda (The Throne)

Population: The Karda is the home of the Flayer Guard and the Znir Pact barracks. Orcs, ogres, minotaurs, and gnolls make up the bulk of the population, while the troop of war trolls form an impressive (if small) segment of it. Aside from the soldiers, goblins and orcs can be seen performing menial tasks and civilian jobs. Foreigners are not welcome here, and few humans or other easterners are visible.

Character: For all that it can be seen as a center for trade, Graywall is a military outpost on the border of Droaam and Breland, and the Karda is the fortress at the heart of the town. High, thick walls surround the district. Foot soldiers patrol the streets, and harpies and gargoyles circle overhead. The gates are open, but the minotaur sentries question any who wish to enter, demanding to know the business that takes them to the Karda. Foreigners are treated with suspicion and might be questioned by patrols.

Inns: None.

Taverns: None.

Supplies: None.

Dragonmarked Services: Ghallanda; Jorasco; Kundarak; Orien; Phiarlan; Sivis; Tharashk.

Temples: No public temples, though there are shrines in the barracks.

主要地点：

卡尔达中没有商业场所。有一座服务于士兵的磨坊，没有其他公共设施。它是一座堡垒，有劫掠者卫队和辛尔豺狼人的兵营，武器库以及在面对围攻时需要保卫城堡的其他设施。

KEY LOCATIONS

No businesses are in the Karda. There is a grist mill for the soldiers, but no public amenities. It is a fortress, with barracks for the Flayer Guard and Znir Pact, armories, and other facilities needed to hold the citadel in the face of a siege.

城堡：这是艾克索切莱尔权力的所在地，是灰墙最奇特的建筑。这位灵吸怪亲自设计了这座城堡，它的墙壁纹理分明且圆润; 有时它们闪烁着一种虹彩的光泽。五十名精英士兵驻扎在塔内，它被加固以抵御围攻。艾克索切莱尔的宝座位于第三层，他大部分时间都坐在这个宏伟的座位上，聆听着城镇的思想，策划着对瑟奥瑞特领主的不可思议的复仇。

The Keep: The seat of Xorchylic's power, the Keep is the strangest building in Graywall. The mind flayer took a personal hand in its design, and its walls are whorled and rounded; at times they gleam with an iridescent sheen. Fifty elite troops are garrisoned within the tower, and it is fortified to withstand a siege. Xorchylic's throne sits on the third floor, and he spends most of his time in this great seat, listening to the thoughts of the town and plotting an incomprehensible revenge against the lords of Xoriat.

最后之塔：这是灰墙的法院。对于许多出现在法官面前的人来说，这也是他们见到的最后一个地方。这座塔既是监狱，也是酷刑中心。卓姆的法律不认为监禁是一种改过自新的形式，囚犯只有在等待转移、行刑或在角斗场中服刑时才会被关押（这可能持续几天或几周）。在广场上举行公开处决，而在塔周围也排列着雕像——这些是被美杜莎法官石化的受害者，留下来警示其他人。通常，法官会切除石化受害者的右手，以确保如果受害者被恢复，至少也是残缺的。

The Last Tower: This is the courthouse of Graywall. It is also the last place many of those who come before a magistrate ever see. The tower serves both as a prison and as a center for torture. Droaamish law doesn't believe in incarceration as a form of rehabilitation, and prisoners are only held if they are waiting for transfer, execution, or serving a sentence in the arena (which can last days or weeks). Public executions are held in the plaza, and statues are also arranged around the tower—these are victims petrified by the medusa magistrate and left as a warning to others. Typically, the justice has the right hand of a petrified victim removed, to ensure that if the victim is restored it is at least maimed.

冒险引子：

✦ 玩家角色受雇于营救一名石化的受害者（可能是一位朋友，或者是从他们的国家搜集到重要信息的间谍），该受害者位于卡尔达的庭院中。他们必须在石像展览园中识别目标，然后艾科索切莱尔和他的劫掠者卫队的注视下，将雕像从卡尔达中移出，或在卡尔达内进行解除折磨的仪式！

ADVENTURE HOOKS

✦ The PCs are hired to rescue a petrified victim (possibly a friend, or a spy from their nation who has learned vital information) from the courtyard of the Karda. They have to identify the target among the stone menagerie and then either remove the statue from the Karda or perform the Remove Affliction ritual in the Karda—all beneath the watchful eye of Xorchylic and his Flayer Guard!

深门

灰墙建在科拉什卡尔的废墟之上，而达坎地精的堡垒深深地延伸到地下。历史的碎片表明，最终打破达坎防线的攻击来自城堡下方。没有人知道达坎隧道下面到底是什么。几乎所有通往深处的入口都已封闭，除了在血石中有一个受到严密保护的通道......深门。艾克索切莱尔曾经允许冒险者进入深处，出于自己的原因；玩家角色也可能会得知通往深处的一条无人防守的通道。深门之下的情况仍然是个谜。说书人编织着异变魔军团跟地精军团激战，以及太古深龙躺在达坎黄金顶上的故事。事实可能奇妙的多，或也可能可怕的多。

The Deep Door (Khyras)

Graywall is built atop the ruins of Korash Khaar, and the fortress of the Dhakaani goblins stretched deep beneath the earth. The fragments of history suggest that the final attack that broke the Dhakaani defenses came from beneath the citadel. No one living knows just what lies below the Dhakaani tunnels. Almost all the entrances to the depths have been sealed, save for a heavily guarded passage in Bloodstone . . . the Deep Door. Xorchylic has allowed adventurers to enter the depths before, for reasons of his own; it's also possible that PCs might learn of an unguarded passage to the deep tunnels. What lies beyond the Deep Door remains a mystery. Storytellers spin tales of aberrant legions locked in combat with goblin ghosts and of deep wyrms atop piles of Dhakaani gold. The truth might be more wondrous still, or infinitely more terrible.

石坑

灰墙是一个正在建设中的城镇。向东，采石场的伤痕深深地刻在坚硬的土地上，而西边则是新建筑物的粗糙基础——额外的海德拉宿舍和新的防御工事。虽然大多数劳动力的成员在城镇内有住处，但有些人正在等待这些营房的建设。此外，每周都有新的怪物来到灰墙寻找机会。卓姆仍然是一个正在发展中的国家。许多人对他们的暴君般的军阀的统治感到不满，他们希望通过直接为女儿服务——或者至少为灰墙之主服务——来实现更伟大的事业。因此，石坑周围是一个由临时工人和那些希望加入他们行列的难民组成的帐篷城镇。

The Stone Pits

Graywall is a town in progress. To the east, the scars of quarries run deep into the stony ground, while the west holds the rough foundations of new buildings—additional hydra halls and new fortifications. Although most of the members of the labor force have bunks within the town, some are awaiting the construction of these barracks. In addition, new monsters are coming to Graywall every week in search of opportunities. Droaam is still a nation in progress. Many of its people still chafe under the rule of their tyrannical warlords, and they hope to achieve greater things by serving the Daughters directly—or at least by serving the Lord of Graywall. As such, the Stone Pits are surrounded by a tent town made up of both temporary workers and those refugees who hope to join their ranks.

灰墙内的势力

在灰墙，许多派系都在运作。尽管女儿们将卓姆打造成一个国家，但城市内部的部落和种族联系仍然是一个强大的力量。雄心勃勃的军阀，如Tzaryan Rrac或Stone Queen，可能在城市中有他们自己的特工。然后还有间谍，布鲁兰的强盗，以及从整个科瓦雷招募的逃兵或战争罪犯组成的团体。以下是一些组织的描述，但这只是冰山一角。

FORCES WITHIN GRAYWALL

Many factions are at work in Graywall. Although the Daughters forged Droaam into a nation, tribal and racial ties are still a powerful force within the town. Ambitious warlords such as Tzaryan Rrac or the Queen of Stone might have their own operatives in the town. And then there are the spies, Brelish bandits, and groups of deserters or war criminals drawn from across Khorvaire. Here are descriptions of a few organizations, but this is just the tip of the iceberg.

劫掠者卫队

领袖：Korgel Blackblade（雄性战争巨魔battle champion）

其他显要成员：Jalira Stormtongue（邪恶女性血火鸟妖warlord11），Salan（邪恶雄性鼠人游荡者）

劫掠者卫队是卓姆的精锐士兵，负责维持卓姆的和平，但他们随时准备面对来自布鲁兰的挑战，或者发动对东方王国的攻击。大多数卫兵是兽人和牛头人，还有一些食人魔和巨魔（对于他们种族的水准来说非常聪明，但仍然用于蛮力行动）。劫掠者卫队有许多特殊部队。Jalira Stormtongue指挥一队石像鬼和鸟妖弓箭手，充当侦察员和空中支援；尽管她在其他方面与11级的血火鸟妖相同，但Jalira的火焰之歌造成雷电伤害。Salan指挥着一队十只小型鼠人；他们都具有+3的潜行和感知加成，在混合形态以外的形态中看起来像地精。劫掠者卫队的真正核心是由索拉·麦尼娅Sora Maenya训练的二十只战争巨魔组成的小队。这些可怕的士兵由Korgel Blackblade指挥，他是一位技艺高超且强大的战争巨魔，挥舞着一把由Szalas Jal锻造的魔法大剑。

劫掠者卫队的每个成员都对他们的灵吸怪主人深具忠诚，这既是因为爱国主义，也是因为心灵的制约。他们是经验丰富的战士，在战斗中使用有智慧的战术；期望兽人和食人魔是一群蠢货的玩家将会感到惊讶。

The Flayer Guard

Leader: Korgel Blackblade (male war troll battle champion)

Other Notables: Jalira Stormtongue (evil female bloodfire harpy warlord 11), Salan (evil male wererat rogue)

The Flayer Guard, elite soldiers of Droaam, keep the peace in Droaam, but they stand ever ready to face a challenge from Breland—or to launch an attack against the eastern kingdoms. The majority of the guards are orcs and minotaurs, along with a few ogres and trolls (remarkably intelligent for their kind, but still saved for brute force operations). The Flayer Guard has a number of special units. Jalira Stormtongue commands a squad of gargoyles and harpy archers who serve as scouts and air support; while she is otherwise identical to an 11th-level bloodfire harpy, Jalira's burning song deals lightning and thunder damage. Salan commands a squad of ten small wererats; all possess a +3 bonus to Stealth and Perception, and they appear as goblins when not in hybrid form. The true core of the Flayer Guard is the team of twenty war trolls, a unit trained by Sora Maenya. These fearsome soldiers are under the command of Korgel Blackblade, a war troll of terrifying skill and power, who wields a magic greatsword forged by Szalas Jal.

Each member of the Flayer Guard is utterly devoted to the group's illithid master, due equally to patriotism and psychic conditioning. They are veteran soldiers and use intelligent tactics in combat; players expecting orcs and ogres to be bumbling buffoons are in for an unpleasant surprise.

萨拉释克家族

领袖：Baron Kundran Torrn（半兽人男性）

其他显要成员：Kel'Ashta（邪恶兽人男性），Mor'Ashta（邪恶兽人bloodrager），Jana Velderan（女性人类游侠8），Lagraa（半兽人女性）

在灰墙内，尤其是在卡拉巴斯内，萨拉释克是一支强大的力量。在Baron Kundran Torrn的领导下，家族已经与城镇内的兽人建立了联系。通过他在挑战角斗场中的慷慨和壮举，Kundran还赢得了灰墙许多其他居民的尊重。在灰墙的领地是许多不同业务的中心，包括在该地区的勘探，雇佣和训练怪物在东方担任雇佣兵和劳工，以及找到任何需要找到的东西。Kel'Ashta是雇佣兵的人才猎头，与灰墙角斗场有很好的联系。Mor'Ashta指挥着看守飞地并在Calabas维持秩序的家族卫队——这是一支由20名兽人掠夺者、狂战士和战士组成的混合部队，以及目前现场的任何怪物雇佣兵。Jana Velderan是主要的审订员。由于她的种族，Jana在灰墙面临了一些挑战，但她擅长自己的工作，并与许多当地土匪建立了联系。

Lagraa是领地的精神顾问，她的性质决定了这里可能发生的长期故事。她可能是护门者德鲁伊的信徒，如果是这样，她可以召唤玩家角色的萨拉释克继承人来对抗城镇下方的畸变和恐怖。然而，她也可能是黄泉巨龙的邪恶女祭司，慢慢地在领地内传播她有毒的信仰，并召唤黑暗中的恐怖。如果是这样，萨拉释克的飞地最终可能会成为一场史诗冒险的中心，因为Lagraa和她的门徒释放了一位达异变魔领主！这种疯狂会污染整个萨拉释克家族，还是已经被局限在灰墙之内？

House Tharashk

Leader: Baron Kundran Torrn (male half-orc)

Other Notables: Kel'Ashta (evil male orc), Mor'Ashta (evil male orc bloodrager), Jana Velderan (female human ranger 8), Lagraa (female half-orc)

House Tharashk is a powerful force within Graywall, especially within the Calabas. Under Baron Kundran Torrn, the house has reached out to orcs across the town. Through his generosity and feats in the challenge rings, Kundran has also earned the respect of many of the other inhabitants of Graywall. The Graywall enclave is the hub of a number of different operations, including prospecting within the region, employing and training monsters to serve as mercenaries and laborers in the east, and the usual work of finding anything that needs to be found. Kel'Ashta is a talent scout for mercenaries, who is well connected with the Graywall arena. Mor'Ashta commands the house guard that minds the enclave and maintains order in the Calabas—a mixed force of 20 orc raiders, berserkers, and warriors, along with any monstrous mercenaries currently on hand. And Jana Velderan is the chief inquisitive. Jana has faced challenges in Graywall due to her race, but she's good at what she does and has made contacts with many of the local bandits.

Lagraa is the spiritual counselor of the enclave, and her nature determines what sort of long-range stories might evolve here. She might be an adept of the Gatekeeper druids, in which case she can call on PC Tharashk heirs to fight aberrations and terrors beneath the town. However, she could be an evil priestess of the Dragon Below, slowing spreading her poisonous beliefs throughout the enclave and calling up horrors from the darkness. If this is the case, the Tharashk enclave could ultimately become the hub for an epic adventure, since Lagraa and her disciples release a daelkyr lord! Will this madness taint all House Tharashk, or has it been contained in Graywall?

红帽子

领袖：Gurb Bonecrow，又称乌鸦（男性地精法师5级）

其他显要成员：Reka the Razor（邪恶女性狗头人slyblade rogue5）

受到同名精类生物的故事启发，这个团伙由地精和狗头人组成，它的活动范围在小灰墙。几千年来，较小的种族一直受到更大、更强大的生物的压迫。

红帽子致力于为这些侮辱复仇。他们基本都在享受伏击食人魔和巨魔的乐趣，但特别针对那些虐待哥布林或狗头人的人，无论他们是当地人还是外国人。到目前为止，他们还没有激起劫掠者卫队的回应，但这可能只是时间问题。红帽子来自多个部落。他们秘密行动，藏在面具和他们那染血的红色帽子后面。该团伙由Gurb Bonecrow领导，他是一位才华横溢的地精法师，戴上面具时称自己为"乌鸦"；他的首席副手是Reka the Razor，一位格外致命的slyblade.。

The Redcaps

Leader: Gurb Bonecrow, also known as the Raven

(male goblin hexer wizard 5)

Other Notables: Reka the Razor (evil female kobold slyblade rogue 5)

Inspired by stories of the fey of the same name, this gang is made up of goblins and kobolds, and it operates out of Little Graywall. For thousands of years, the smaller races have been oppressed by larger, more powerful creatures.

The Redcaps are dedicated to taking vengeance for these indignities. They enjoy ambushing ogres and trolls on general principle, but specifically target anyone who abuses goblins or kobolds, whether they are locals or foreigners. So far they have not provoked a response from the Flayer Guard, but this could be a matter of time. The Redcaps are drawn from multiple tribes. They operate in secret, hiding behind masks and their blood-soaked red caps. The group is led by Gurb Bonecrow, a talented goblin wizard who calls himself "the Raven" when masked; his chief lieutenant is Reka the Razor, an especially deadly slyblade.

老骨头

领袖：ZarisLornath（邪恶男性人类warlord)）

其他名人：Cayna（邪恶女性矮人death master),Desh Anarn（邪恶男性骷髅tomb guardian bodyguard）

老骨头是资深的坎纳斯士兵——爱国者和沃尔之血的狂热信者。然而，他们不是翡翠利爪的成员；他们得出结论，翡翠利爪的领袖并没有真正致力于沃尔之血的信条，也没有牢记坎纳斯的最大利益。他们梦想着有一天把奸诈的坎埃斯三世赶下台，同时清洗寻求者seekers的队伍

虽然他们的目标可能会吸引坎纳斯，但老骨头并不是正直的英雄。他们中的许多人是战犯，因为在上次战争中异常残忍而被通缉。牧师Caynau因屠杀战俘以便使他们成为不死士兵而臭名昭著，Zaris Lornath多次下令对平民发动袭击。老骨头们相信恐怖是一种工具，死灵术是一种武器，他们不惜一切代价确保坎纳斯至上。老骨头可能是对抗翡翠利爪的盟友，但他们充其量只是令人不安的盟友。

由DM来决定Zaris和Cayna的力量。他们最大的盟友是Desh Anarn，一个堕落的沃尔之血冠军，作为一个有知觉的坟墓守护者guardian bodyguard回来了。让Anarn带着这种力量回来的情况仍然是个谜；这可能是由于他对沃尔之血的极大信仰，也可能他是伊兰蒂斯·沃尔的创造物——一个被派去观察这个分裂组织的间谍，看看是否可以利用它。

The Old Bones

Leader: Zaris Lornath (evil male human warlord)

Other Notables: Cayna (evil female dwarf death master), Desh Anarn (evil male skeleton tomb guardian bodyguard)

The Old Bones are veteran Karrnathi soldiers— patriots and fanatical followers of the Blood of Vol. However, they are not members of the Order of the Emerald Claw; they have concluded that the leaders of the Emerald Claw are not truly devoted to the principles of the Blood of Vol, nor do they have the best interests of Karrnath at heart. They dream of some day driving the treacherous Kaius III from the throne and simultaneously cleansing the ranks of the Seekers.

Although their goals might appeal to Karrns, the Old Bones are not virtuous champions. Many of them are war criminals who are wanted for acts of extraordinary cruelty during the Last War. The cleric Cayna is infamous for slaughtering prisoners of war so that he could raise them as undead soldiers, and Zaris Lornath ordered many attacks on civilian populations. The Old Bones believe in terror as a tool and necromancy as a weapon, and they stop at nothing to ensure Karrnathi supremacy. The Old Bones could be allies against the Emerald Claw, but they are disturbing allies at best.

It's up to the DM to decide just how powerful Zaris and Cayna are. Their greatest ally is Desh Anarn, a fallen champion of the Blood of Vol returned as a sentient tomb guardian. The circumstances that allowed Anarn to return with such power remain a mystery; this could be due to his great faith in the Blood of Vol, or he could be a creation of Erandis Vol—a spy sent to watch this splinter group to see if it can be used.

其他显要成员：Gray Garrak（男性stran gler rogue 8）

嘲弄是谋杀和背叛的君主，而剥皮者相信他指引着他们的双手。这个怪物帮派积极地捕捉东方的人，杀死那些愚蠢到离开卡拉巴斯的外国人——有时甚至冒险进入卡拉巴斯的墙壁，以对付一个坦率的银焰信徒或杜哈拉的圣武士。在可能的情况下，剥皮者会活捉受害者，以便他们可以被缓慢拷问。这个帮派的领袖是Zurin Souldrinker；他的吞噬灵魂的力量在造成伤害的同时剥离了受害者的皮肤，可以用于无意识或无助的目标。他的强有力的助手是Gray Garrak，一个具有惊人杀戮天赋的恶毒bugbear。

Other Notables: Gray Garrak (male bugbear stran

gler rogue 8)

The Mockery is the Sovereign of murder and treachery, and the Skinners believe that he guides their hands. This gang of monsters actively preys on the people of the east, killing foreigners foolish enough to leave the safety of the Calabas—and occasionally venturing within its walls to target an outspoken follower of the Silver Flame or paladin of Dol Arrah. 自由之剑

领袖：Tars Faldren（男性人类demagogue)

其他显要成员：Tolri（邪恶女性提夫林法师/ devastator），Norn（男性矮人bodyguard)

自由之剑秘密活动在不用刻意卡包的边境内。Tars Faldren是该组织的创始成员之一，当他在布鲁兰变得太危险时，他决定在灰墙建立一个藏身之地——一个在逃成员的避难所，以及一个从中公开传播他们的信息的基地。Faldren自997 YK以来一直在灰墙工作，并一直在向外国政府请愿，与龙纹家族和科兰贝尔克纳克编年史交谈，以及以其他方式传播他的反君主制信息。在公开场合，他淡化了自由之剑的暴力行为，但在幕后，他利用自己的地位为他在布布鲁兰的盟友获取非法商品。

Faldren的藏身之地隐藏在血石区中。他带着一些自由之剑的成员，包括新成员、保镖和一些已经曝光并在布鲁兰被通缉的人。其中最重要的是Tolri，一位以制作引发爆炸的卷轴而臭名昭著的提夫林法师。

The Swords of Liberty

Leader: Tars Faldren (male human demagogue)

Other Notables: Tolri (evil female tiefling wizard/ devastator), Norn (male dwarf bodyguard)

The Swords of Liberty operate in secret within the borders of Breland. Tars Faldren is one of the founding members of the organization, and when it became too dangerous for him to remain in Breland, he decided to establish a safe house in Graywall—a haven for Swords on the run and a base of operations from which to openly spread their message. Faldren has been operating out of Graywall since 997 YK, and he has been petitioning foreign governments, speaking with dragonmarked houses and the Korranberg Chronicle, and otherwise spreading his antimonarchy message. Publicly he downplays the violent actions of the Swords of Liberty, but behind the scenes he uses his position to acquire illegal goods for use by his allies in Breland.

Faldren's safe house is hidden within Bloodstone. He is accompanied by a number of Swords, including new recruits, bodyguards, and a few others who have been exposed and are wanted in Breland. Chief among these is Tolri, a tiefling wizard infamous for her skill at crafting ritual scrolls that set off terrible explosions.

PCs who sympathize with the Swords of Liberty can come to Graywall to make contact with Tars, or to receive missions or equipment. Alternatively, the King's Citadel could ask the party to infiltrate the Swords and shut them down—but only after learning about upcoming plans and Faldren's contacts within Breland.
