进阶职业：树之友The Tree-friend（龙杂志 #307，page 73）

尽管一只树精关爱着整座森林，但她只能相连一棵与她分享灵魂的树。任何协助保护林地之人都是这只难以捉摸的精灵生物的朋友，但保护树精之树的人更是她的恩人和拯救者。

树之友教团的成员承担了保护一棵或多棵树精之树以及周围林地的责任。一位树之友对森林、特别是对树精的的奉献是无与伦比的。当永恒的林地处于危险之中，她像秋叶飘落般悄无声息地移动，用伟大橡木之力打击敌人。感受到树之友怒火的人有祸了，因为这就是大自然本身的愤怒。

树之友通常组织成松散的小组来监视特定区域。小组的各个成员独立运作，根据需要协助其他成员。较大的树之友社群称为树枝；较小的社群则称为嫩枝。最年长的树之友通常担任领导职务，指挥看守行动、监督设置防御陷阱。

大多树之友曾经是游侠或德鲁伊，但也有自然神祗的牧师。居住在森林里的野蛮人也倾向于保护野生土地，他们可能会发现该进阶职业是个有趣的提升选项。

如果一个NPC树之友认为入侵者有意伤害她所保护的东西，她可能会保持在视线之外跟随入侵者几英里，然后把监视入侵者的任务交给下一位树枝或嫩枝成员。她在战斗中会利用森林的掩护来最大限度地发挥优势，从隐蔽处打击以尽量减少损失。

生命骰：d10

进阶条件

为了成为一位树之友，角色必须满足以下要求

阵营：混乱善良，混乱中立或中立善良

技能：自然知识8级，生存8级

特殊：候选人必须成功拯救至少一棵与树精相连的树免于毁坏或腐化。

职业技能

树之友的本职技能：攀爬，手艺（任意），脱逃，躲藏，自然知识，聆听，潜行，搜索 ，察言观色，侦查，生存和游泳。

每级技能点数： ４＋智力调整值

职业特性

擅长武器与防具：树之友不会获得任何武器，盔甲与盾牌的擅长。

四季变换 SeasonalChange（特异）：

如树精一样，树之友的体貌也会随着季节而变化。春夏之季，树之友的头发像翠绿多叶的藤蔓。秋日树之友的发丝变得细腻，如秋叶般变成红色或金色。冬天像雪一样白。树之友的性格也会随季节而变化——春夏时节的她往往喜气洋洋，秋日喜好安静甚至有轻微的抑郁，冬季清冷又疏离。树之友在与四季变换相匹配的野外环境中，躲藏检定获得+4环境加值。

自然的疗愈 Nature's Healing（超自然）：树之友与林地如此和谐，她可以利用它们的能量来疗愈自己。她每天可以治疗的生命值，等于她的魅力调整值乘以她的树之友等级，并可以选择分多次使用治疗能力。树之友必须与活着的树木有身体接触才能使用此能力。

植物交谈（类法术）：2级起，树之友获得植物交谈的能力，每日次数等于角色的树之友职业等级，施法者等级如同角色等级的术士。

魅惑人类（类法术）：3级起，树之友获得魅惑人类的能力，每日次数等于角色的树之友职业等级，施法者等级如同角色等级的术士。

融身入林（类法术）：4级起，树之友获得融身入林的能力，每日次数等于角色的树之友职业等级，施法者等级如同角色等级的术士。

树精转化（特异）：  5级起，树之友的生物类型变成精类生物，使得她变得更像她所倾慕的树精。她的容貌变得更加娇嫩、美丽和飘逸，耳朵也变得有点尖。如果她还没有昏暗视觉，获得昏暗视觉特性。她的魅力属性+2加值。

（维基资料）

   守护神祗：西凡纳斯Sylvanus，希昂莉亚Shiallia

   西凡纳斯的树之友通常扮演林地哨兵的角色，因为她认为破坏植被树木是无法救赎的罪恶。西凡纳斯的圣兽，树精，被尊为神祗意志的活生生的化身，是所有树之友的完美榜样。

   希昂莉亚的树之友们比起西凡纳斯的树之友们更活泼和母性，但对自身任务也同样投入。她通过教团来保护野生动物，帮助那些迷失的人找到回家的路。

The Tree-friend

Although she cares for the forest as a whole, a dryad is bound to a single tree with which she shares her soul. Anyone who helps protect the woodlands is a friend to the elusive fey creature, but one who protects the dryad's own tree is also a personal benefactor and savior.

A tree-friend is a member of a cult that has assumed responsibility for the protection of one or more dryad trees as well as the surrounding woodlands. A tree-friend's devotion to the woodlands in general and to dryads in particular is unsurpassed. When the eternal glades are in peril, he moves as quietly as a leaf falling in autumn and strikes with the strength of the mighty oak. Woe to those who feel the wrath of a tree-friend, for it is like the fury of nature itself.

Tree-friends are often organized into loose bands that monitor specific areas. Each member of the group operates independently but assists the others as needed. Larger groups of tree-friends are called branches; smaller ones are known as twigs. The eldest tree-friends usually assume positions of leadership, directing watches and overseeing the laying of defensive traps and snares.

Most tree-friends were once rangers or druids, but some were clerics of nature deities. Forest-dwelling barbarians also tend to be protective of the wild lands, so they too might find this prestige class an intriguing option for advancement.

If an NPC tree-friend believes that intruders intend harm to his charges, he might follow them for miles, keeping just out of sight, then turn the task of monitoring them over to the next member of his branch or twig. In combat, he uses the cover of the forest to best advantage, striking from concealment to minimize losses.

Requirements

To qualify to become a Tree-Friend, a character must fulfill all the following criteria:

Alignment: Chaotic good, chaotic neutral, or neutral good

Skills: Survival 8 ranks, Knowledge (Nature) 8 ranks

Special: The candidate must have saved at least one tree to which a dryad was bound from destruction or corruption.

Class Skills

The tree-friend's class skills are Climb, Craft (any), Escape Artist, Hide, Knowledge (nature), Listen, Move Silently, Search, Sense Motive, Spot, Survival, and Swim.

Skill Points at Each Level: 4 + Int modifier.

Class Features

All the following are class features of the tree-friend prestige class.

Weapon and Armor Proficiency: A tree-friend gains no new weapon, armor, or shield proficiencies.

Seasonal Change (Ex): Like the dryad, a tree-friend undergoes changes in physical appearance with the seasons. During the spring and summer, a tree-friend's hair resembles green, leafy vines. In the fall, it turns red or gold and becomes thinner and finer, like autumn leaves. In winter, it is white as snow. The tree-friend's personality also changes with the seasons - he tends to be mirthful and joyous in spring and summer, quiet and even slightly depressed in autumn, and cold and removed in winter. When in a wilderness environment that matches the seasonal change the tree-friend is experiencing, the tree-friend gains a +4 circumstance bonus to Hide checks.

Nature's Healing (Su): A tree-friend is so in tune with the woodlands that he can tap their energy to heal himself. He can cure a number of hit points each day equal to his Charisma bonus times his tree-friend level, and he can spread this healing out among several uses. The tree-friend must be in physical contact with a living tree to use this ability.

Speak with Plants (Sp): At 2nd level, the tree-friend gains the ability to use speak with plants once per day per tree-friend level as a sorcerer of a level equal to the tree-friend's character level.

Charm Person (Sp): Beginning at 3rd level, a tree friend can use charm person once per day per tree-friend level as a sorcerer of a level equal to the tree-friend's character level.

Tree Stride (Sp): Upon reaching 4th level, the tree-friend gains the ability to use tree stride once per day per tree-friend level as a sorcerer of a level equal to the tree-friend's character level.

Dryad Apotheosis (Ex): At 5th level, the tree-friend's type changes to fey, making him even more like the dryads he so admires. His features become more delicate, beautiful, and ethereal, and his ears acquire slight points. He gains low-light vision if he did not have it already, and his Charisma score increases by +2.
