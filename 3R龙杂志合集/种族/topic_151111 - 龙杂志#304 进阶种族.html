进阶种族 - 让你的角色变得更好

Prestige Races - Changing your Character for the Better

by Greg Dent and Brannon Hollingsworth, illustrated by Chris Appel.

DND规则创造了一个英雄们可以在技能和力量上获得迅速提升的世界。在这个世界中，生存冒险和冒险精神是一种真正的学习经历，而这会导致其产生各种身体和心理的变化。在这样的世界里，生物甚至可以通过获得经验来改变它们的存在形式。在Bastion Press出版的Oathbound战役设定集中，引入了"进阶种族"的概念，这一概念将允许角色做到上述的内容：通过支付经验值(XP)，任何生物都可以改变其存在形式，从而获得新的能力。

进阶种族的表现方式成千上万，对身体和心理的任何变化都可以是进阶种族的变化结果。无论是微妙的"智力"属性的提升，还是相当明显的长出一对翅膀。大多数变化不是支离破碎的，而是存在一个特定的专注方向。人不能简单的直接把自己变成一个元素生物，但能够通过一系列较小的变化来慢慢朝着元素生物转变。这些转变的路径被称为专注点(foci)，而这些专注点则是区分不同进阶种族的重要方式。

The DUNGEONS & DRAGONS rules create a world where heroes can experience rapid advances in their skills and power. Surviving adventure and risk-taking are real learning experiences that cause physical and mental change. In this kind of world, creatures might even alter their forms by gaining experience. The Oathbound campaign seffing, produced by Bastion Press, presented the concept of "prestige races," allowing characters to do just that. By expending XP, any creature can alter its form and gain new abilities.

A prestige race can manifest itself in many thousands of different ways. Any change to the body or mind can be the result of a prestige race alteration, whether it is subtle, such as an increased Intelligence score, or quite obvious, such as the growth of a pair of wings. Most changes are not piecemeal but the result of focusing on a particular prestige race focus. A human cannot simply turn himself into an elemental, but he might slowly progress in that direction over a series of smaller changes. These pathways are known as foci, and are the chief method of categorizing prestige races.

获取进阶种族变化Gaining a Prestige Race Alteration

如果想获取进阶种族的变化，生物必须首先选择专长"自我塑形"。当具有自我塑形专长的生物想要获取进阶种族的变化时，它会立刻消耗XP。这就像使用金币购买物品一样，只不过购买的是能力而货币是XP。然后，该生物必须花费相应的时间以将XP引导进它的全新形态。生物不必立刻这么做，如果有这种需要，这个过程可以推迟。如果生物获取了多个进阶种族的变化，那么它必须分别为每个变化引导一次XP。

To gain a prestige race alteration, a creature must first take the Sculpt Self feat. When a creature with the Sculpt Self feat wants to purchase a prestige race alteration, it immediately expends the XP. It must then meditate for the time necessary to channel the XP into altering its form. A creature need not do so immediately, and the process can be delayed as long as the creature wishes. If the creature purchased multiple prestige race alterations, it must meditate for each separately.

自我塑形 Sculpt Self [一般]

你具有改变你存在本质的能力。

效果：你能够花费XP以获取进阶种族的变化。你不能因为获取进阶种族变化花费的XP导致等级降低。

Sculpt Self [General]

You have the ability to modify the essence of your being.

Benefit: You may spend XP to gain prestige race alterations. You cannot spend the XP for a prestige race alteration if that expenditure would reduce your level.

生物必须为每次获取进阶种族变化的XP成本(每1,000 XP)冥想一天，假定生物每天冥想约8小时。但你不能通过每天冥想超过8小时来加快这个过程。只要生物每天冥想满足8小时，就不会因为些许小事或轻微活动破坏这个过程，但战斗、施法、使用魔法物品以及其他精神或身体上强度较大的活动会破坏当天的冥想。

与施法者制造魔法物品不同，中断只会破坏当天的工作，而不是整个过程。因此，生物可以通过伪装来获取尽可能长的冥想时间，从而获得进阶种族的变化。

一旦获得，进阶种族变化不能被夺走。即使是由于能量吸取和其他效果而失去等级也永远不会使你失去进阶种族变化，类似的，这些变化不能被反制法术。进阶种族变化的大部分效果被视为特异能力(Ex)，但由进阶种族变化授予的任何施法效果被视为超自然能力(Su)。你不能多次选择相同的进阶种族变化。

A creature must meditate for one day per 1,000 XP of the cost of the prestige race alteration, and the creature is presumed to spend about 8 hours meditating each day. You cannot rush the process by meditating more than 8 hours each day. Small interruptions or light activity do not disrupt this process so long as the creature spends about 8 hours a day meditating, but fighting, casting spells, using magic items, or other mentally or physically intensive activities ruin the day's meditation.

Unlike a spellcaster creating a magic item, interruptions merely ruin the day's work, not the whole process. Thus, a creature can fake as long as it wishes to gain the prestige race alteration.

Once gained, the prestige race alteration cannot be taken away. Loss of levels due to energy drain and other effects never take away a prestige race alteration, and they cannot be dispelled. Most of the effects of a prestige race alteration are considered extraordinary abilities, but any spellcasting ability granted by the prestige race alteration should be considered supernatural. You cannot take the same prestige race alteration more than once.

进阶种族效果的叠加Stacking Prestige Race Benefits

进阶种族变化所提供的效果将会遵循《地下城主指南》(DMG)中所描述的加值类型的正常叠加规则。比如说，一个生物先从进阶种族变化中获得了+4的天生防御加值，并有一个+3的天生防御护符，那么它只会将进阶种族变化中所获得的+4天生防御加值叠加在其AC上。但如果它有一个+5的天生防御护符，那么它就会获得护符的+5加值而不是进阶种族变化的+4.

Stacking Prestige Race Benefits

The benefits provided by prestige race alterations follow the normal stacking rules for bonus types described in the Dungeon Master's Guide. For instance, if a creature has a +4 natural armor bonus from a prestige race alteration and an amulet of natural armor +3, it gains only the prestige race alteration's natural armor bonus to its AC. If it had an amulet of armor +5, it would instead gain the benefit from the amulet and not the prestige race alteration.

进阶种族的专注点Prestige Race Foci

进阶种族变化可以分作若干个不同的专注点(foci)。每个进阶种族的专注点代表了一种特定的进阶种族变化的路径。这些路径类似于专长(feat)，但角色不需要选择"自我塑形"以外的专长来获得进阶种族变化，他们只需要消耗适当数量的XP和时间。

Prestige race alterations can be grouped into different foci. A prestige race focus represents a particular path of prestige race alterations. This path is much like a feat free, bul characters do not need to take feats to gain prestige race alterations; they need only spend the proper amount of XP and time.

Those who focus on the mineral path hold an affinity with the earth and its deepest foundations. The very bones of the world call out to them and hold mysteries that appeal to theri innermost selves. Progressing along this path, characters slowly experience changes to their bodies, allowing them to gain powers, abilities, and traits related to the deepest parts of the earth.

Kindled Creature [Flame]

Kindled creatures have felt the stirrings of the flame deep within their souls for as long as they can remember. Within the furnaces of their hearts, a tiny fire has sprung into existence and sears outward, eternally unquenchable. Kindled creatures' dispositions become strained; their tempers shorten as the spark within them grows. Typically, the creature's skin takes on a reddish-orange hue, and their hair and eyes begin to roil with an inner flame.

Prerequisite: Sculpt Self feat.

Benefit: The creature gains low-light vision and fire resistance 5.

Special: This prestige race alteration is unavailable to creatures with wave prestige race alterations.

Cost: 1,600 XP.

Ember Creature [Flame]

Ember creatures have discovered how to fan the flame within them; they have learned how to draw upon its inherent power in order to react with speed and precision. An ember creature's skin becomes opaque, dark, and cracked, with the vague hints of a smoldering fire lying deep beneath its surface. All of the hair on an ember creature's body falls away or vanishes in a puff of acrid smoke, and its eyes darken, leaving only two tiny pinpricks of gleaming light.

Prerequisites: Kindled Creature, Sculpt Self feat.

Benefit: The creature gains fire resistance 10 and the ability to cast produce flame at will as a 3rd-level sorcerer.

进阶种族与挑战级数Prestige Races and CR

给怪物或NPC赋予进阶种族变化与赋予它们经验和职业等级不同。相反，你应该像对待拥有魔法物品的怪物或NPC一样考虑拥有进阶种族的怪物或NPC。如果你将进阶种族变化的XP成本乘以5，你就得到了一个没有空间限制且具有这些效果的魔法物品的成本。

如果你从你分配给NPC装备的金钱中减去这个成本，你就不必担心它如何对NPC的挑战级数(CR)或遭遇等级(EL)产生负面作用，这与你给NPC一个魔法物品一样。如果进阶种族变化是NPC装备或怪物可用财宝的补充，你必须像给生物额外魔法物品一样仔细考虑它如何影响遭遇。请注意，如果生物有许多部位仍然空余能够穿戴魔法物品，你可能需要考虑将进阶种族变化的金币价值减半：这是由于一个有许多部位能够穿戴魔法物品的生物不会花费资源购买没有空间限制的物品。

Giving monsters or NPCs prestige race alterations is not the same as giving them XP and levels of classes. Instead, think of a monster or NPC with a prestige race as you would a monster or NPC with a magic item. If you multiply the XP cost of a prestige race alteration by 5, you get the cost of a magic item with no space limitation and those qualities.

If you subtract this cost from the money you devote to the NPC's gear, you shouldn't have to worry about how it will negatively affect the NPC's CR or the difficulty of the encounter any more than normal when giving an NPC a magic item. If the prestige race alteration is in addition to an NPC's gear or a monster's usable treasure, you have to consider how that affects the encounter as carefully as if you were giving the creature extra magic items. Note that if the creature hasplenty of item slots open, you might want to consider halving the gold piece value of the prestige race alterations: A creature with many item slots open would not expend the resources to buy an item with no space limitation.

创造你独特的进阶种族变化Creating Your Own Prestige Race Alterations

进阶种族的能力可以带来几乎无限的效果，但XP成本可能会使某些变化不太可能被使用。本文介绍了价值高达11,980XP的进阶种族变化。这接近许多玩家愿意为这些效果支付的上限，并且这么高的XP成本也将进阶种族变化限制在12级或更高等级的角色。在设计您自己的进阶种族变化时，像技能加值这样的低成本好处最有可能被玩家选择和使用。

确定进阶种族变化的XP成本在概念上很简单，但数学计算可能会变得复杂。首先，确定每个能力的单独花费，就好像它们都是没有空间限制的独立魔法物品的品质一样。你可能想因为这些"物品"永远不能被偷走或摧毁而增加一个临时的价格，但要考虑它们也永远不能交给另一个可能需要它们的队伍成员，或者被卖掉以购买更好的物品，并且需要一个专长来获得其效果。将所有这些价格总计起来。一旦你确定了以金币计算的总价，将其除以5就等于其XP成本。请注意，如果进阶种族变化相互之间存在成长性，就像"专注于矿物"中不断增加的天生防御加值一样，你需要在将这加到你对进阶种族变化品质总金币成本的计算中之前，从新效果的成本中减去先前效果的成本。

The possible effects of prestige race abilities are nearly limitless, but the XP cost can make certain alterations unlikely to be used. This article presents prestige race alterations worth up to 11,980 XP. This approaches the upper limit of what many players will be willing to pay for the benefits, and it limits the prestige race alteration to characters of 12th level or higher. When designing your own prestige race alterations, cheap benefits like skill bonuses are the most likely to be chosen and used by players.

Determining the XP cost of a prestige race alteration is simple in concept, but the math can become complicated. First, determine the cost of each of the granted abilities as though they were all qualities of separate magic items that have no space limitation. You might want to add an ad-hoc price increase due to the fact that the "items" can never be stolen or destroyed, but consider that they can also never be given to another party member who might need them or be sold to buy a better item, and that a feat is required to gain the benefits. Total all those prices. Once you have determined the total price in gold pieces, divide that cost by 5 to learn the XP cost. Note that if the prestige race alterations build upon one another, as in the case of the mineral focus's increasing natural armor benefit, you'll need to subtract the cost of the previous benefit from the cost ofthe new benefit before adding that to your calculations for the total gold piece cost of the prestige race alteration's qualities.
