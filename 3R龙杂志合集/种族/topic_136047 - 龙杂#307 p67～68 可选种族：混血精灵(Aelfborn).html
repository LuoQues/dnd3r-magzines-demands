前言：本人于2022年7月中旬因友人开3r团而入坑dnd，目前仅仅入坑三个月整，因此文中部分dnd专有名词可能存在部分错译现象，敬请各位谅解与斧正。那么以下是正文：

混血精灵(Aelfborn)：

(https://morloch.shadowbaneemulator.com/images/1/1d/Aelfborn-1.png)

精灵是个充满魔力的种族。他们寿命漫长、美貌异于常人并且有着与生俱来的对魔法能力的倾向性，关于他们的一切都在诉说着流淌在他们血液中的魔法力量。人类缺乏这些天赋，但他们不甘屈居于此，反而用让其他富有天赋的种族感到陌生的热情来弥补并实现。人类用巨大的热情去追求任何他们没能得到的东西。因此，人类能与精灵平分秋色。

然而，光是种族的对等并不意味着他们能很好地结合，混血精灵就是活生生的证明。他们是精灵与人类结合后生下的不育混血儿，他们的混血血统不够稳定的缺陷表现在精神而非肉体之上。

像精灵这样富有天赋的种族并不意味着一定能够拥有人类培养出的激情与动力。两者的结合虽然相当强大，但很少有混血精灵能稳定到运用这些能力。

  • 性格：许多混血精灵给人的印象是冷漠与不可信。由于他们往往会被出生地所放逐又无法建立自己的长期社区，因此他们很早就明白了很少有人能包容或是接纳他们，并且几乎不会有人信任他们。更不幸的是即使在同类中情况也是如此。因此，他们经常会自轻自贱并用恶意来构建心防。但如果有人能成为混血精灵的朋友的话，那么混血精灵将与他情同手足，成为他最坚定的盟友。

    混血精灵让人捉摸不定，因为他们的混血血统不稳定又反复无常，而这会让他们走向疯狂。他们中的每一个都是疯狂的。这种疯狂表现方式与他们人类的那一面一样千差万别。有些混血精灵产生了多重人格，有些屈服于杀戮的冲动，还有些渴望看到烈焰熊熊燃烧——无论那燃烧的是人还是物。这种疯狂能以任意形式表现出来，但不变的是这会让他们的生活困难重重。尽管他们并没有一直被疯狂所掌控，但这种疯狂的发作有着显而易见的随机性和严重性，这就足以让混血精灵成为身边的人的危险因素了。

  • 体型与外貌：混血精灵的体格轻灵而优美，但他们比精灵更强壮，他们身高5～6英尺，体重在90～180磅之间。他们有着与精灵父母相同的蜜汁般的肤色。然而他们的发色几乎能是任何颜色。骨白色，墨黑色，任何深褐色、金黄色的纯色或是混合颜色都能成为他们头顶的颜色。混血精灵在15岁左右成年。然而，他们反复无常的混血血统终究对他们造成了严重的不良影响，他们比父母中的任何一方都衰老得更快；绝大多数混血精灵在80岁之前就会死去。

    混血精灵最为显著的身体特征便是他们的纹身。身为一个混血精灵，在清醒时分的每分每秒都必须与疯狂搏斗，即使在睡梦之中，许多混血精灵也不得安生。在很久以前，这些饱受折磨的混血精灵了解到把某些特定的魔法符文永久性地绘制在脸上能有助于减轻他们所承受的疯狂。如今所有混血精灵一出生就会立即得到这些纹身。任何没有这些纹身的罕见混血儿都会被无情地猎杀，除了那些最为腐化堕落之地。

  • 与其它种族的关系：可悲的是，混血精灵几乎没有朋友。精灵认为他们是被人类的缺点所玷污的最差的精灵族群。绝大多数其他种族则是轻率到根本不相信他们受难于精神错乱。在所有种族中，半兽人在与这些有纹身的家伙打交道时所感到的不适最为轻微。混血精灵偶发的狂野行为常常充满了暴力与破坏性，除了半兽人之外很少有能够理解他们的。

  • 阵营：尽管他们的疯狂意味着他们明显倾向于混乱，但混血精灵过于我行我素以至于没有种族阵营倾向。每个混血精灵都会以自己的方式横行于世。

  • 居住地：混血精灵在建立社群时会面临与阴影人(Shade)一样的困境。不育使他们无法繁育自己的人民，因此他们必须持续不断地将同类聚集起来以建立得以生存的城镇或村庄。然而与阴影人相比，他们还面临一个额外的困境：短寿。当阴影人能花数个世纪的时间将更多同类吸引到一个区域时，混血精灵的世代更替甚至比人类还快。在这世上，他们只是单纯没有足够的时间来更替老人与死者。小型的混血精灵社群的确存在，但它们很难延续超过几代人，而且这种小型社群的存在往往是例外多于常态。比起自己的居住地，绝大多数混血精灵都甘愿独自游荡在异族的居住地上。

  • 宗教：虽然混血精灵没有全族信奉的主神，但许多混血精灵都狂热地选择了欧拜•亥信仰。大自然的广阔与在宁静笼罩下的野蛮暴虐，是对混血精灵状态的完美比喻，或者说，是一种发自内心的感受。

  • 语言：混血精灵说通用语与精灵语。

  • 姓名：混血精灵有着人类或是精灵的姓名，这取决于他们在哪里出生与成长。然而在成年后他们通常会起个假名并开始自己的生活。由于大部分混血精灵都在荒野中长大，这些假名通常以自然为主题。诸如绿守(Green Warden)、树友(Tree Friend)、狼群(Wolf Pack)和强爪(Strong Claw)这样的名号便能证明这一点。

  • 冒险者：没有社群愿意长期接纳混血精灵，硬要说的话，漂泊一生往往是混血精灵的唯一选择。他们的独行天性与不断旅行的需要使得他们非常适合成为冒险者。

混血精灵种族特性：

  • +2敏捷, -2体质, +2智力, -2感知：反复无常的混血血统给予了混血精灵不可思议的灵敏头脑与灵活身体，但这份天赋同时也损害了身心健康。

  • 奖励专长：混血精灵保留了一些人类父母的成就之火，但它有些黯淡。该奖励专长必须与混血精灵的职业相匹配。战士必须从战士奖励专长中选取；施法者必须从超魔专长、物品制造专长或法术相关专长中选取；野蛮人获得一个狂暴专长（在《荒野统治者》（Masters of the Wild）中介绍）；以此类推。任何职业的混血精灵都能选取本职技能的技能专攻作为该奖励专长。

  • 奖励技能专攻：同理，混血精灵也保留了一些人类的学习能力，但混血血统使它变得混杂不清。每5级混血精灵都能免费获得技能专攻专长。他能将该专长应用于任何等级至少为一的技能上。

  • 中型体型：由于体型中型，混血精灵在体型上没有任何加值或者减值。

  • 混血精灵的基本陆地速度为30英尺。

  • 昏暗视觉：在星光，月光，火把或类似的弱光情况下，混血精灵的视觉距离是人类的2倍。同时，在这种状况下，他们依然能分辨色彩和物体的细节。

  • 技能：混血精灵在搜索、聆听和侦察技能检定上具有+1种族加值。

  • 精灵之血（Elven Blood）：在与种族相关的所有能力和效果上，混血精灵都视同精灵。例如，混血精灵能像精灵一样拥有使用精灵武器和特殊魔法物品的特有能力。

  • 疯狂（Madness）：混血精灵升级时会承受感知属性的内在罚值。每2级，该罚值增加1（1级时为0，2级时为-1，4级时为-2，以此类推）。

    你可以考虑使用《CoC d20》(Call of Cthulhu)中的理智值规则来替换上述规则。它的统计范围会略大一些，但系统更加灵活。如果你决定这么做，请务必阅读有关理智值损失和精神伤害(mental hardness)规则的附录。当混血精灵的纹身被抑制或无效时，移除这些精神伤害效果。

  • 纹身（Tattoos）（Su）：混血精灵的神秘纹身缓解了他们内在疯狂的部分影响。

    如果他们的魔法纹身被压制（比如通过反魔场(Antimagic Field)时），他们必须每轮进行一次DC=10的意志豁免检定，否则便受到困惑术(confusion)的效果直到纹身的魔力恢复。该能力是超自然能力。

  • 天生使用语言：通用语（Common），精灵语（Elven）。额外语言：所有语言（除了秘密语言，如德鲁伊语言）。

  • 天赋职业：巡林者。巡林者的独行侠生活对混血精灵来说很有吸引力。动物不会像类人生物那样随意评判他们，而且在荒野之中他们的疯狂也会被忽略。由于天生聪慧，绝大多数混血精灵施法者都是术士而非法师。

以下为原文

Aelfborn

Elves are a magical race. Their long life, their alien beauty, their natural inclination toward wizardry, everything about them tells of the magic running in their veins. Humans lack these gifts, but instead of settling for being inferior, they compensate with a passion for accomplishment that is strange to a more gifted race. What humans are not granted, they pursue with intense fire. And so these two races stand in balance.

However, just because the racial nations balance does not mean that they mix well, and the aelfborn are living proof of that. Sterile half-breeds born from the union between elf and human, the aelfborn demonstrate the unstable mix of their heritage not in physical defect, but in mental.

Races as gifted as the elves were not meant to contain the passion and drive humans developed. And while such a thing can be a powerful combination, few aelfborn are stable enough to use of this unique combination.

Personality: Many aelfborn come across as cold and distrustful. Oftentimes cast from their birth lands and unable to form long-lasting communities of their own, aelfborn learn early that few tolerate them or welcome them, and almost none trust them. Unfortunately, this is true even of their own kind. Thus aelfborn often weave a protective shell of spite and scorn about themselves. Should someone prove a friend however, the aelfborn will be as that person's brother and staunchest ally.

Aelfborn are difficult to predict because the mix of their blood is unstable and volatile, and it drives the aelfborn mad. Each and every one of them is insane. How this manifests is as varied as the aelfborn's human side. Some develop multiple personalities, some succumb to homicidal fits, others yearn to see fire, no matter what or who is burning. It could be anything, but invariably the condition makes life among others difficult. Although not continually in the grip of madness, the apparent randomness, and severity, of these attacks are enough to make aelfborn a danger to those around them.

Physical Appearance: Lithe and sleek in build, but more robust than elves, aelfborn range from 5 to 6 feet tall and weigh anywhere from 90 to 180 pounds. They bear the homogenous honeyed complexion of their elven parents. Their hair, however, can be nearly any color. Bone white, jet-black, and all shades and combinations of brunette, blonde, and red crown the aelfborn. They reach maturity sometime after the age of 15. However, the volatile mix in their blood ultimately takes its toll on all aelfborn, who age faster than either of their parents; most die before reaching their 80th birthday.

The most distinguishing physical trait of the aelfborn is their tattoos. To be aelfborn is to wrestle with madness every waking minute, and many of the sleeping ones as well. Long ago, these tortured people learned that certain mystical runes, when permanently drawn on the face, help abate the madness aelfborn suffer. Now all aelfborn receive these marks immediately upon birth. The rare half-breed without them is hunted without quarter in all but the most depraved lands.

Relations: Aelfborn, sadly, are almost friendless. Elves see them as the worst of their own species further sullied with human frailties. Most of the other races simply don't trust the mental instability that all aelfborn suffer. Of all the races it is the half-orcs who suffer the least discomfort in dealing with the tattooed folk. The wildness of aelfborn behavior is sporadic and often violent or destructive, something the half-orcs appreciate like few others.

Alignment: Despite their apparent lean toward chaos that insanity might suggest, the aelfborn are simply far too individualistic to have a racial alignment leaning. Each makes her way through the world in her own way.

Aelfborn Lands: Aelfborn have the same difficulties establishing communities that shades do. Sterility prevents them from propagating their own people, so they must continually gather others of their ilk together in order to make a town or village survive. However, they face an additional difficulty that shades do not: short lifespans. While shades could spend centuries drawing more of their own kind into an area, aelfborn generations are even shorter than those of humans. They simply do not have time enough in the world to replace their elderly and dead. There are small aelfborn communes to be sure, but they rarely last more than a few generations, and are more the exception than the rule. Most aelfborn are content to wander other races' lands on their own.

Religion: While there is no chief racial deity for the aelfborn, many of them turn to Obad-Hai with a fanatical ferocity. The grand scope of nature, serenity enveloping savage brutality, is the perfect analogy for the aelfborn condition, or so the devout feel.

Languages: Aelfborn speak Common and Elven.

Names: Aelfborn have either human or elven names, depending on where they were born and raised. Often, however, they'll take up a pseudonym upon reaching adulthood and setting out on their own. Since so many of them wind up in the wilds, these often have a nature theme. Titles such as Green Warden, Tree Friend, Wolf Pack, and Strong Claw are demonstrative of this.

Adventurers: With no community willing to take them for long, if at all, life on the road is often the only option for an aelfborn. Their independent nature and constant need to travel makes them well suited to be adventurers.

Aelfborn Racial Traits

●+2 Dexterity, -2 Constitution, +2 Intelligence, -2 Wisdom: The volatile mix of racial blood grants the aelfborn incredible adroitness in mind and body, but the strain of these gifts leaves both weak at the same time as well.

●Bonus Feat: Aelfborn retain some of the fire for accomplishment from their human parent, but it does not burn quite as brightly. This bonus feat must be specifically related to the aelfborn's class. Fighters must select it from their bonus feat list; spellcasters must take a metamagic, spell related, or item creation feat; barbarians gain a rage feat (as described in Masters of the Wild); and so on. An aelfborn of any class can take Skill Focus in a class skill as this feat.

●Bonus Skill Focus: Again, the aelfborn retain some of humanity's drive for learning. but their mixed blood muddles their abilities. Every 5th level the aelfborn gains the Skill Focus feat for free. He can assign the feat to any skill in which he has at least one rank.

●Medium-size: As Medium-size creatures, aelfborn have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

●Aelfborn have a base speed of 30 feet.

●Low-light Vision: Aelfborn can see twice as far as humans in starlight, torchlight, moonlight, and similar conditions of poor illumination. They retain the ability to distinguish color and detail under these conditions.

●Skills: +1 racial bonus on Listen, Search, and Spot checks.

●Elven Blood: For all special abilities and effects, an aelfborn is considered an elf. Aelfborn, for example, can use elven weapons and magic items with racially specific elven powers as if they were elves.

●Madness: Aelfborn gain an inherent penalty to Wisdom as they advance in level. At every 2nd level, this penalty increases by one (0 at 1st level, -1 at 2nd level, -2 at 4th level, and so on).

If you have access to them, you might consider using the sanity rules from Call of Cthulhu instead of the above mechanic. The bookkeeping is a tad more extensive, but the system allows for greater flexibility. If you decide to take this option, be sure to read the appendix dealing with mental hardness rules when using sanity loss in a fantasy game. Remove this mental hardness whenever their tattoos are dispelled or suppressed.

●Tattoos: The mystical tattoos of the Aelfborn stave off some of the effects of their inherent madness.

Should their magical tattoos ever be suppressed (through an anti-magic field, they must make a Will save (DC=10) every round or suffer the effects of a confusion spell until the tattoo's magic returns. This is a supernatural ability.

●Automatic Languages: Common and Elven. Bonus Languages: Any (other than secret languages, such as Druidic).

●Favored Class: Ranger. The lonely life of the ranger holds much appeal to aelfborn. Animals rarely judge like humanoids do, and in the wilderness their insanity can go ignored. Despite their natural intellect, most aelfborn spellcasters are sorcerers, not wizards.
